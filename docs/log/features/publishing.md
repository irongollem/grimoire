Shipped features in the **Publishing & Export** area, newest first. Part of the Grimoire feature log — see the [log index](../index.md).

- [x] **Illustrated character-sheet export — recovered, calibrated, shipped** (part of EPIC #422) — the June work stranded on `feat/illustrated-character-sheets` (5 fully-illustrated themes × front/back × A4/Letter as baked-PNG plates with live-data overlay fields) was merged into main and finished. The blocker that killed the first attempt — "the designer couldn't align the text" — turned out to be three compounding causes, all fixed: (1) a CSS-porting bug where the base `.fld` rule's higher specificity silently overrode every per-section layout rule (death saves stacked into the painted heading, etc.); (2) percentage padding resolving against the page instead of the field; (3) the design handoff's false claim that all five fronts share one coordinate grid — every AI-generated plate has its own geometry, so all **20 (theme × side × size) coordinate sets were individually calibrated by eye** via a new DEV-only `/dev/sheet-calibration` route (URL-driven fixture at 100% scale, debug box outlines) and a headless screenshot loop. Per-theme art quirks are honored (temp HP inside the painted heart where one exists and a tiny "temp" caption where none does, gothic's values sitting on its skull glyphs in light ink, arch-shaped vs hanging-scroll portrait masks). Along the way: weapon-attack math extracted from `PlayerCombatTab` into tested `src/lib/weaponAttack.ts` and wired into the sheet so attacks print real bonus/damage instead of "—" (vault items supplied per context: DM `useItems`, player `usePlayerVisibleItems`), a 1px page-height drift between the PDF capture and the illustrated render fixed, `sheetData.ts` pinned by 17 tests, and the export panel hardened (per-member remount, px-CSS → Tailwind). 42 sheet-related tests green.
