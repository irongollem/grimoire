Combat in Dungeon Grimoire, from the DM's side.

Heroes and monsters in turn order, with hit points and armour class. Click a monster, type the damage, done. Monsters attack straight from their stat block: roll to hit, roll damage, apply it to the hero they struck. Press Next Turn and the table moves on.

The player's side is one post back.

Music: the Grimoire theme, made in the app's own soundboard.
