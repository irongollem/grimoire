Combat in Dungeon Grimoire, from the player's side.

Roll your own initiative from your phone. When your DM starts the fight you see the round, who is up and how everyone is holding out. Your phone tells you when it's your turn, and when a monster hits you, your health bar drops on your own screen.

Next up: the DM's side, running the whole fight from one screen.
