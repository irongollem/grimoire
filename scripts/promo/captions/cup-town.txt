A checkered hillside, and a town in a teacup below it.

Read it aloud: "You are lying on grass. The grass is checkered, green and a paler green, in neat squares the size of a saucer. Below the hill there is a town inside a teacup. The teacup is the size of a town. Someone is ringing a bell that sounds, if you listen carefully, exactly the same every time."

Steal this: leave a wicker basket in the grass, placed neatly, as if left for the party. Inside is a warm thermos, one cup for each of them, and a folded card: "Drink it while it is warm." Each player reads the card in their own handwriting. If anyone asks who wrote it: "You did. You don't remember writing it. You did."

This is the opening of Late in the Kind Country, a setting we are writing in Dungeon Grimoire. Stay as long as you like.
