At the edge of the marsh, a cottage with a lantern lit on its porch.

Read it aloud: "At the edge of the marsh is a small wooden cottage with a lantern lit on its porch. Beside the cottage is a dock. At the dock is a raft made of three pieces of toast. And on the dock is a child."

Steal this: the child is Crumble, the ferryman, a little Brewling of patched tea-cloth with a long pole and a fare basket. The fare is one small thing you no longer need: a button, a pebble, a ribbon, an old key. He keeps every fare in a labelled jar on the dock. Ask each player what their character gives up, and why. It is the best character question you will ask all session.

This is a Book Nook: a painted marsh with its raft, its lantern and its ferryman moved by code. You can make one for any place in your campaign in Dungeon Grimoire and play it on the TV at your table. From Late in the Kind Country, the setting we are writing in Dungeon Grimoire. Link in bio.
