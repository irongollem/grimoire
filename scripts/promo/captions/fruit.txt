Each tree here bears one kind of memory.

Steal this: in the Mirror Orchard every fruit holds a memory, and whoever eats one sees through other eyes for a moment. The plums are sweet at the front and bitter at the back, the way an apology is. The apples taste of a kitchen no one is in. Let a player take a bite, describe a memory that is not theirs, and do not say whose it is.

This is a Book Nook: a painted orchard with its branches, its glowing fruit and its falling pear moved by code. You can make one for any place in your campaign in Dungeon Grimoire and play it on the TV at your table. From Late in the Kind Country, the setting we are writing in Dungeon Grimoire. Link in bio.
