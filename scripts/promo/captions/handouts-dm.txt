Handouts in Dungeon Grimoire, from the DM's side.

Press New handout and pick what it is written on: a letter, a notice, a journal page or a plain page. It opens ready to write and saves itself as you type. When it is ready, press Give to players, tick who gets it or pick the whole party, and it lands on their phones at once.

The player's side went up earlier this week.

Music: the Grimoire theme, made in the app's own soundboard.
