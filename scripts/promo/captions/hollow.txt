In the orchard, the hedge holds the last of the light. Every dusk, they walk.

Steal this: the Hollow Ones were Bisques, people of porcelain, who took in too many memories that were not their own. Now they walk the orchard every dusk in single file, humming below what the ear can hear, wanting nothing. A guest may walk beside them, if they keep a respectful distance. Let your players hear the hum before they see the line, and do not explain it.

This is a Book Nook: a painted orchard with its walkers and its falling petals moved by code. You can make one for any place in your campaign in Dungeon Grimoire and play it on the TV at your table. From Late in the Kind Country, the setting we are writing in Dungeon Grimoire. Link in bio.

The song is the Settling Lullaby, from the Late in the Kind Country songbook.
