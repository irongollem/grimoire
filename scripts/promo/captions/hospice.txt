Out on the marsh, great teapots are moored together. This one is a house for the end of the leaf.

Read it aloud: "Inside is a single round room with a low ceiling. A hearth at the back. A long wooden table down the middle, set with mismatched chairs. Cups on the table, dozens, in different patterns, all in use."

Steal this: Brewlings are people made like tea bags, cloth and leaves and warm water, and as they grow old their cloth turns pale and the leaves show through. The Ferryman's Hospice is where they spend their last days, by the window, watching the water. There is always a cup poured for whoever comes next. If your players sit down, let the old one by the window tell them one thing he remembers.

This is a Book Nook: a painted room with its steam, its light and its rocking chair moved by code. You can make one for any place in your campaign in Dungeon Grimoire and play it on the TV at your table. From Late in the Kind Country, the setting we are writing in Dungeon Grimoire. Link in bio.
