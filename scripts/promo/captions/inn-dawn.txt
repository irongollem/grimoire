Dawn at the Pulled Sugar Inn.

A quiet morning in Sugarwell, the candy city. The kettle is on, and the candle from last night still burns. Play it at your table the next time the party wakes up at the inn.

Save it for your next session.

The fire and the bells are from Dungeon Grimoire's sound library, and you can make music like this in its soundboard.
