The Pulled Sugar Inn, an hour before the first guest comes down.

Read it aloud: "The kettle is already singing on its trivet, and a single candle still burns beside it, though the window has gone rose and gold. Somewhere across the terraces a bell strikes three times. Downstairs, Floss is brewing the best caramel breakfast tea in Sugarwell."

Steal this: the music box in Floss's parlour was made decades ago by Master Cantor of the Conservatory. Late in the evening it plays a slowed version of the city's bell-note, and nobody recognises the tune. Mention it to Cantor, and he sits down at his keyboard and plays it at its true tempo.
