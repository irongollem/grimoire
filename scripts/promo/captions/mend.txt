In the White Quarter, every crack is a memory survived.

Steal this: the Bisque are people of glazed porcelain, hollow inside. When one of them cracks, a Mender reads the crack aloud, naming the memory it survived, and then dresses it in gold leaf. In their city, gilded cracks are read the way another country reads a record of service. Give a Bisque in your game one gold seam, and wait for a player to ask what it is for.

This is a Book Nook: a painted room with its light, its dust and its Mender's gold moved by code. You can make one for any place in your campaign in Dungeon Grimoire and play it on the TV at your table. From Late in the Kind Country, the setting we are writing in Dungeon Grimoire. Link in bio.

The song is the Mending Song, from the Late in the Kind Country songbook.
