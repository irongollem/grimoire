At the end of the hall, a chest. No lock. No trap that you can see.

Read it aloud: "The hall ends in a shaft of grey daylight from a grate far above. In the middle of the light stands a chest: old oak, black iron bands, no lock on the hasp. A few gold coins lie on the flagstones around it, as if someone left in a hurry."

Steal this: the coins are bait, and so is the missing lock. A mimic takes the shape its prey will trust, and this one has learned that adventurers trust a chest that looks too easy to be a trap. A character who studies it closely can make a Wisdom (Perception) check: on a success they see the hasp rise and fall, very slowly, like breath.

This is a Book Nook: a painted hall with its torches, its dust and its chest moved by code. You can make one for any place in your campaign in Dungeon Grimoire and play it on the TV at your table. Link in bio.

The room tone, the creak and the bite play from Dungeon Grimoire's soundboard, and the battle music is "The mammoth thunders forth", made with its music generator.
