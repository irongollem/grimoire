A night in the cells, and the jailer has stepped out.

Read it aloud: "The torch outside your cell has burned down to a sputter. The jailer's chair is pushed back from the table, his bread half eaten, his tankard still wet. And sitting in the straw, just out of reach, is his dog. It has the keys in its mouth. It is wagging its tail at you."

Steal this: the dog's name is Turnkey, and it has seen a hundred prisoners come and go. It will bring the keys to whoever offers it the jailer's bread, but it has to be coaxed close to the bars first. Ask for a Wisdom (Animal Handling) check, and let a clever idea beat the dice. If the party gets out, Turnkey follows them, and the jailer will want his dog back.
