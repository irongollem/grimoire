Quests in Dungeon Grimoire, from the DM's side.

Open a quest and the whole story is on one screen, with the read-aloud for the scene in front of the party in the drawer beside it. Tick Party here and your players see the scene the moment the party arrives. When they move on, take a route, see the payoff it fires, and advance.

The player's side went up last night.

Music: the Grimoire theme, made in the app's own soundboard.
