Quests in Dungeon Grimoire, from the player's side.

When your DM shares a quest, it appears in your Journal under Quest Log. As the party plays, the story fills in on your own phone: each scene your DM reveals joins Story so far, Happening now marks where you stand, and the objectives tick off as you complete them. You never refresh.
