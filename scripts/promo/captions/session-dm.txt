Game night in Dungeon Grimoire, from the DM's side.

Arrange your dashboard the way you run a table. Press Start session and your screen turns to the table, with a live rail that keeps the time. Quests open ready to run, with a thread for every path. And the Deck sits under every page: press a music pad and it plays, press another and the first fades out, and effects play over the music without stopping it.

The player's side went up earlier this week.

Music: the Grimoire theme, made in the app's own soundboard.
