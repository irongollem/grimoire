Character sheets in Dungeon Grimoire, from the player's side.

Your whole hero on your phone: hit points, armour class and all six abilities at a glance. Tap an ability to roll a check, hold a saving throw for advantage. Your weapon has its own attack and damage buttons, casting a spell spends the slot, and your gear sits on a paper doll.

Next up: the DM's side, with the whole party on one screen.

Music: the Grimoire theme, made in the app's own soundboard.
