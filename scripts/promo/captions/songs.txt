The songs page in Dungeon Grimoire.

Our youngest player is five and can't read yet, but he knows every song from the table. So we made him a page of pictures: tap one and it plays. When the DM ticks Players can listen on a playlist, it turns up on his screen by itself, and pinned to the home screen it opens as its own app, Grimoire Songs.

The songs are from Late in the Kind Country, the setting we are writing in Dungeon Grimoire.
