Steal this monster: the Blinkhill.

A hill like any other hill, and a good place to eat lunch, until the afternoon it opens an eye. It blinks once an hour, and the hillside changes while it is closed. It sleeps until someone on it starts a fight, and a good song puts it back to sleep.

Read-aloud text, how to run it and the full 2024 stat block are in the slides. From Late in the Kind Country, the setting we are writing in Dungeon Grimoire.

Save it for your next session.
