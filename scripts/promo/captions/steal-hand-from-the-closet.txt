Steal this monster: the Hand from the Closet.

A wardrobe door standing open a crack in the middle of nowhere, and a pale hand on an arm far too long. A nightmare given just enough shape to fight: it grabs, it drags you toward the dark, and the fight ends the moment someone thinks to shut the door.

Read-aloud text, how to run it and the full 2024 stat block are in the slides. From Late in the Kind Country, the setting we are writing in Dungeon Grimoire.
