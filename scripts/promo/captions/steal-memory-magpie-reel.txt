Thieves who give everything back. Eventually.

Memory-Magpies take whatever your party leaves lying around. Three afternoons later it turns up in the fork of a tree, in the path of whoever they decided should find it.

The read-aloud, the stat block and a printable card are in the post on our profile. Steal them for your next session.
