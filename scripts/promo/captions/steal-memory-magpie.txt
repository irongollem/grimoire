Steal this creature: the Memory-Magpies.

They do not hoard, they curate. Whatever they take from your party turns up three afternoons later, in the fork of a tree, in the path of whoever they decided should find it.

Read-aloud text, how to run them and the full stat block are in the slides. Written in the Scriptorium in Dungeon Grimoire, where a page keeps your monster's stats up to date.
