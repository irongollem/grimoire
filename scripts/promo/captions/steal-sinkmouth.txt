Steal this monster: the Sinkmouth.

A round hole by the roadside, lined with pink fondant and ringed with peppermint teeth, smelling of vanilla and warm bread. It only wants a taste of whatever you have: whatever is in your hand if it is sweet, and your hand if it has been sweet recently. Feed it, and it leaves you alone for a day.

Read-aloud text, how to run it and the full 2024 stat block are in the slides. From Late in the Kind Country, the setting we are writing in Dungeon Grimoire.
