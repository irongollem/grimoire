Steal this monster: the Snuffer.

The road's night-watch. It comes down the road after the last lamp is lit and puts out every flame left burning, one by one, very politely. Hand it a lit candle and it carries the flame away until dawn. Fight it and you fight in the dark.

Read-aloud text, how to run it and the full 2024 stat block are in the slides. From Late in the Kind Country, the setting we are writing in Dungeon Grimoire.
