You all meet in a tavern. The fire is warm, and the ale is cheap.

Steal this: the stranger in the corner has been waiting for one of your characters in particular. Before anyone sits down, ask the table which of them owes someone a favour. Whoever answers first is the one the stranger looks up at.

This is a Book Nook: a painted common room with its fire, its lantern and its stranger moved by code. You can make one for any place in your campaign in Dungeon Grimoire and play it on the TV at your table. Link in bio.
