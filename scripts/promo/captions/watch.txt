The fire has burned low. Your watch is nearly over. Out in the trees, something is very still.

Steal this: give the player on watch one detail before the roll, not after it: the insects have stopped. Whatever they roll, the eyes are already there. A good Perception check tells them how many; a bad one tells them only that the fire is too low to see by.

This is a Book Nook: a painted night wood with its fire, its smoke and its eyes moved by code. You can make one for any place in your campaign in Dungeon Grimoire and play it on the TV at your table. Link in bio.
