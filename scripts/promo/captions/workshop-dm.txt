Crafting in Dungeon Grimoire, from the DM's side.

Your players can invent things. Here Chicory wants a night-light for the bakery loft, made from leftover candy-glass, and it arrives as a full recipe with the rules written out. Nothing joins your world until you approve it. Every recipe in your campaign lives in the Workshop: what it takes, how hard it is and what it makes. When the party finds the notebook, share the recipe with whoever earned it.

Music: the Grimoire theme, made in the app's own soundboard.
