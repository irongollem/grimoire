Crafting in Dungeon Grimoire, from the player's side.

When your DM shares a recipe, it lands in your Workshop, and every card tells you what you have and what you still need. Open the workbench and there is a dish for every ingredient, already filled from your pack. The ledger spells out the DC, the time, your roll and your tools. Then roll: a success puts the result in your pack, and a miss costs your main ingredient a step of quality.

Next up: the DM's side, where your players' inventions become recipes.

Music: the Grimoire theme, made in the app's own soundboard.
