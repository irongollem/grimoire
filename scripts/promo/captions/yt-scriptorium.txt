The Scriptorium is where you write the things your table can hold in Dungeon Grimoire: a book of chapters to run your adventure from, and handouts your players read on their own phones.

In three and a half minutes: starting a book from a template, writing on the page, pictures behind the text or pinned to a corner, the Illuminator for edges and colour, handing a letter to your players, and printing the whole book as a real PDF.
