## 1. Overview

This policy explains how cancellations, refunds, and the EU right of withdrawal apply to paid plans (Grimoire **PRO**) and **AI credit packs**. It supplements our [Terms of Service](/terms) — where this policy and the Terms differ on a billing matter, this policy controls.

## 2. PRO subscription

### Billing and renewal

A PRO subscription is billed in advance — monthly or annually, depending on the plan you choose. It renews automatically at the end of each billing period until you cancel.

### Cancelling

You can cancel at any time from **Billing → Manage subscription** (the Stripe customer portal). Cancellation takes effect at the **end of the current billing period**: you keep PRO access until then, and you are not charged again.

### Refunds on subscriptions

We do not provide partial refunds for the unused portion of a billing period once it has started, **except** (a) for your statutory right of withdrawal, under which you are refunded on a pro-rata basis for any part of the period not yet supplied (see §4); (b) where otherwise required by law; or (c) at our discretion in cases of genuine error (e.g. an accidental duplicate charge). If you believe you were charged in error, email **<info@dungeongrimoire.com>** within 14 days.

## 3. AI credit packs

AI credit packs are **prepaid, consumable digital goods**.

- **Unused credits** can be refunded on request within **14 days** of purchase, as long as your purchased-credit balance **still covers the full amount of that pack** — i.e. you have not yet spent those credits. Email **<info@dungeongrimoire.com>** with the subject line **"Credit Refund"**.
- **Once you have drawn your purchased-credit balance below the amount of a pack**, that pack is treated as consumed and is non-refundable, because the digital service has been performed (see §4).
- When we refund a pack, the corresponding credits are removed from your balance.
- Credits do not expire.

## 4. EU right of withdrawal (consumers)

If you are a consumer in the EU/EEA, you normally have a **14-day right of withdrawal** on distance purchases.

For **digital content and services**, this right **ends once we begin supplying the service with your prior express consent**. At checkout you are asked to:

1. **expressly request** that we begin providing the digital service (PRO access / AI credits) immediately, and
2. **acknowledge** that you will therefore **lose your 14-day right of withdrawal** once the service has been fully supplied (PRO) or the credits have been used.

Until you have used any credits — or for the part of a PRO period not yet supplied — you may still withdraw by emailing **<info@dungeongrimoire.com>**. This does not affect your statutory rights as a consumer, including remedies for a service that is faulty or not as described.

## 5. How to request a refund

Email **<info@dungeongrimoire.com>** with:

- the email address on your account,
- what you are requesting a refund for (PRO subscription or which credit pack), and
- the approximate date of the charge.

We aim to respond within **5 business days**. Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method via Stripe; the time for the funds to appear depends on your bank or card issuer.

---

## 6. Chargebacks

If you have a billing concern, please contact us first — we can usually resolve it faster than a card dispute. Opening a chargeback without contacting us may result in temporary suspension of the account while the dispute is investigated.
