**Your Account** is your identity across every campaign — not campaign-specific settings. Open it from the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar (click your name, then **Account**), or go straight to `/account`.

The same popover menu also has **Edit display name** (the name other members see in chat and member lists), the DM/Player mode switch, **Install app** (add Grimoire to your home screen, shown when your browser supports it), **Report a bug**, and **Sign out** — those live one click away from Account but aren't part of this page.

## Key ideas

- **Account** — one row: the email address on file, plus a link on to **Billing & Subscription**.
- **Data export** — a GDPR access/portability request you can trigger yourself at any time.
- **Delete account** — permanently removes your account; campaigns you own go with it.

## Downloading your data

1. On the **Download My Data** panel, click **Download my data**.
2. Grimoire builds a single JSON file containing your profile, campaign memberships, subscription and consent records, credit history, journals, notes and preferences, plus a list of every file stored under your account.

API keys and invite links are deliberately left out, so the file is safe to keep but can't be used to sign in anywhere.

## Deleting your account

1. Read the warning: campaigns you own — and everything in them — are deleted, and your players lose access; content you created in other people's campaigns is removed too; billing records are kept in anonymized form as legally required. If you want a campaign to survive you, transfer ownership to another member first (**Campaign Settings → Danger Zone**).
2. Type **DELETE** into the confirmation field.
3. Click **Delete account**.

This cannot be undone.

## What your players see

Nothing here is player-visible — this page is your own account identity, export, and deletion, not a campaign setting. If you delete your account, any campaign you owned disappears for every player in it; a campaign you only played in is unaffected for its other members.

## Tips

> There is no notification-preferences section on this page — email notifications (a shared note, a proposed session date) are opt-out settings each **player** controls individually from their own portal's **Settings** screen (see [Journal, Party & Live Play](#journal-party-live-play)), not something a DM configures from here. As DM, you never receive these emails yourself; they only go out to your players.

- Export your data before deleting your account — deletion has no grace period and no undo.
- Transfer ownership of any campaign you want to keep running before you delete your account; deleting it takes every campaign you own with it.
