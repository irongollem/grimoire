The Billing page (`/billing`) shows your current plan and AI credit balance, and is where you upgrade, manage payment, or buy more credits.

## Plans

Free DMs get the full toolset with per-resource quotas (campaigns, sounds, etc.). **Pro** removes those limits and includes a monthly AI credit allowance. Toggle **Monthly / Annual** billing to compare pricing before upgrading.

Once you're a paying customer, **Manage billing** opens the Stripe customer portal to update payment methods or cancel — cancelling keeps Pro access until the end of the current period rather than cutting it off immediately.

## AI credits

Credits power AI generation across the app — portraits, scenes, stat blocks, and more. Two buckets:

- **Monthly** — included with Pro, resets each billing period, use-it-or-lose-it.
- **Purchased** — bought in packs, never expires, and tops up whenever you need more regardless of plan.

If a pending cancellation would put you over the free plan's limits, a warning lists exactly what will be locked (not deleted) once Pro access ends.
