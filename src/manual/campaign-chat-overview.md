Campaign Chat is a floating panel available from anywhere in the app — a tab on the right edge on desktop, a slide-up sheet on mobile — for in-session messages and mechanical drops, not just talk.

## Sending things, not just messages

Beyond plain text, chat carries structured drops players can act on directly from the message:

- **Item & currency drops** — a player clicks **Claim** or **Grab** to pull the item or coin straight into their inventory.
- **Vendor offers** — the DM (or another player) offers an item for sale; the buyer pays and receives it in one click.
- **Loot chests** — a shared pool of rolled rewards that multiple players can claim from until it's empty.
- **Dice rolls** — shared roll results, visible to the whole party or sent privately.

## Managing the chat

DMs can delete individual messages or clear the whole log with **Delete all**. An unread-message dot appears on the tab when the panel is closed and new messages have arrived.
