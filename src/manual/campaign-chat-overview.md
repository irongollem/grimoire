Campaign Chat is a floating panel available from anywhere in the app — a tab on the right edge on desktop, a slide-up sheet on mobile — for in-session messages and mechanical drops, not just talk. It has no page of its own; open it with the chat tab (desktop) or the chat icon in the top bar (mobile, and in the Player Portal).

## Key ideas

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Whisper | Send a text message or dice roll to one player instead of the whole table. |
| Drop | Something a player can claim directly from the message — an item, currency, or a shared loot chest. |
| Vendor / player offer | A sale offered through chat — from you (DM) or from a player selling to the table. |
| Persona | Speaking in chat as one of your NPCs instead of as yourself. |

## Sending things, not just messages

Beyond plain text, chat carries structured drops and offers players can act on directly from the message:

- **Item drops** — a player clicks **Grab** (or **Grab 1** / **Grab All** on a stacked drop) or **To Stash** to pull the item into their inventory.
- **Currency drops** — a player clicks **Add to Purse** to pull the coin into their inventory.
- **Vendor offers** (you offer an item for sale) — a player clicks **Pay** to buy it and receive it in one click.
- **Player offers** (a player offers one of their own items for sale to the table) — another player clicks **Buy**, or you click **Accept (DM)**.
- **Loot chests** — a shared pool of rolled rewards; each player clicks **Claim** on an item until the chest reads "Chest is empty."
- **Dice rolls** — click the dice icon to open the roller. Results post publicly by default.

## Sending a whisper or a hidden roll

1. Use the **To: Everyone / [player] (whisper)** selector above the message box.
2. Pick a player's name to send a text message only they can see, or a dice roll only they (and you) can see.
3. Picking yourself/the DM as the target on a roll is how a player rolls in secret — you'll see it, they won't share it with the table.

**Critical rolls always post publicly**, even if you whispered the roll — a crit isn't the kind of thing that stays hidden.

## Speaking as an NPC

If you use the **As: [NPC]** picker before sending, your message posts under that NPC's name instead of yours — handy for feeding the party in-character information without spoiling which NPC it came from if they haven't been revealed yet.

## Managing the chat

- Delete an individual message with the trash icon that appears on hover (yours, or anyone's if you're the DM).
- Click **Clear all messages** (DM only) to wipe the whole log — a confirmation dialog asks first, since this can't be undone.

## What your players see

Players get the same panel, minus your DM-only controls (the persona picker, the vendor-offer composer, and Clear all messages). Everything else — drops, offers, whispers, dice — works identically for them. Two things post here automatically and are worth knowing about:

- **NPC reveals announce themselves** while a session is running — showing an NPC to the party posts a line like _"The party encounters Sephek Kaltro."_ Silent while no session is running.
- **"⚔️ The session begins."** posts once when you start a session.

## Tips

> Whispering a roll to yourself is the standard way to run a hidden check (a player rolling Perception against something they might not notice) without a separate mechanism.

- An unread-message dot appears on the closed chat tab/icon when a message arrives while the panel is shut; it clears the moment you open it.
- A stacked item drop's **Grab 1** vs **Grab All** only appears when the drop has more than one of the item — a single item just shows **Grab**.
