Campaign Notes (`/notes`) is a free-form space for anything that doesn't have a dedicated home elsewhere — session recaps, lore you haven't turned into a Quest or Faction yet, secrets you're not ready to reveal, or planning scratch-space.

## Creating a note

Click **New Note** to open the editor. Notes are rich text, so use headings, lists, and bold text to keep longer entries scannable.

Notes are DM-only by default — there's no separate player-visibility toggle here the way there is on NPCs or Custom Rules, so treat this as your private scratchpad rather than a place to share content with the table.

→ _See The Interlude — Downtime_ for one place notes get created automatically — a resolved downtime draw can produce a note as its outcome.
