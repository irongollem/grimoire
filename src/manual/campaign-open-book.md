The Open Book turns your campaign into a book that anyone can read in a browser, without an account. Visitors see a leather cover, chapter tabs and pages they turn. It shows what your whole table has found, never your secrets, and it is read-only.

You find it in **Campaign settings**, on the **Open Book** tab. Only the campaign's owner sees that tab: a co-DM can edit the campaign but cannot publish it.

## Set it up

Press **Set up the Open Book**. That creates the settings only: nothing goes public. Then fill in the steps on the page:

1. **Address.** Where the book will live, at /w/ followed by your own word. Use 1 to 48 lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens. If the word is taken, pick another.
2. **Cover.** A **Title**, a **Line under the title**, the cover **Art** (**Tooled only**, **Framed plate** or **Full cover**, with a portrait picture for the last two) and the **Leather** colour. A preview of the cover sits beside the form.
3. **What goes in.** See below.
4. **Readers.** **Let readers download a PDF**, and **Findable by search engines** (off keeps the book to people you give the address).

The settings save themselves as you type.

## Preview or release

These are two different things.

- **Preview the book** is free for every owner. It opens the real reader with a banner saying it is a preview of your unreleased book, so you see exactly what visitors will see. **End preview** or **Back to settings** leaves it.
- **Review and release** needs **Pro**. Without Pro your settings are kept, and the button stays off. A child account cannot release.

A release is a snapshot. Visitors read the book as it was when you released it. Editing your campaign afterwards changes nothing public until you release again.

When you press **Review and release**, you see a release number and what changes for visitors since the last release, in three columns: **New**, **Revised** and **Taken out** (things taken out return to your table only). You may add **A line for the contents page** (optional). Then press **Release to the world**, or **Not yet** to go back.

Past releases are listed on the right under **Releases**: the live one is marked **Live**, and **Put back** makes an older release the live one again. **Close the book** makes the address stop working at once. Your releases are kept, and **Open the book again** brings it back in one click.

## What goes in

A book never runs ahead of your table. An entry can go in only when every character in the party can read it, and the party is not empty. Anything the whole party cannot see yet is left out, however you tick.

- **Everything the whole party can see** is on by default, so new reveals join the next release by themselves. Switch it off to choose entry by entry.
- The chapters are **The Atlas**, **Folk**, **Factions and Houses**, **The Pantheon**, **Lore and Legend** and **The Chronicle** (your session reports), plus an index of names. Each chapter has a tick box; the list shows how many entries each holds.
- **Choose entries…** opens a picker where you take single entries in or out.

### The eye on a record

On a record's share control (the eye) there is a section **AND BEYOND THE TABLE** with a switch **In the Open Book**. It is live once the whole party can see the record. When it is not, the line under it says whom to add. A change reads "From the next release".

### Bulk on lists

On the NPC list and the faction list, select several entries and use **Add to Open Book** or **Take out of Open Book**.

### Never printed

The settings page lists what stays out of every release, whatever you tick:

- your DM notes and secret blocks,
- quest progress, objectives and clocks,
- map pins, zones and rooms the party has not found,
- a disguised person's true name, until revealed (a disguised person appears under their cover name),
- players' journals and character sheets,
- anything shared with only some of the party.

NPCs appear as a name, a picture and the facts players already know, with no private text.

## The PDF

With **Let readers download a PDF** on, each release can be set as a printable A5 book with a cover, contents, chapter openers, running heads and an index with page numbers. In the release list, press **Export PDF** on a release (it becomes **Replace PDF** and a **PDF** link once made). Readers then see **Download PDF** in the book. Page numbers exist in the PDF only: the web book paginates to the reader's screen.

## The share link

Share the address, which looks like /w/ followed by your word, on your Grimoire site. When someone pastes it into a chat or a social post, it shows a card with your cover and title. The settings page previews that card under **When someone shares the link**. While the book is open, the address is a link on the release page, and it is shown greyed out when the book is closed.
