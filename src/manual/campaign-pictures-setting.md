The **Pictures** section holds two switches that change how pictures look **on your screen only**. Nobody else sees a difference, and your choice is remembered on this device. You will find the section in two places: on your **Account** page (open it from the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar), and on the player **Settings** page.

## Show AI labels

Pictures made with AI carry a small label saying so. Turn **Show AI labels** off to hide those labels on your own screen. Everyone else still sees them.

## Low art

Turn **Low art** on if you would rather read than look. Lists and sheets are then set for text instead of a picture, with no placeholder pictures standing in for missing art. It works the same on DM pages and in the player portal.

### What goes

Low art hides the pictures that belong to a record:

- portraits of NPCs, heroes, party members and companions;
- the pictures of monsters, spells, items and recipes;
- a place's picture, a faction's or pantheon's emblem, and a deity's portrait and symbol;
- the pictures of species, classes and backgrounds;
- the pictures of features, traps and puzzles.

### What stays

Anything that is a picture by nature is left alone: battle maps and the Table screen, tokens, Book Nooks, handouts and Scriptorium books, the gallery and minis, printed cards and the exported character sheet, and the pictures in the combat runner.

### How lists and sheets look

- **Cards** lose their picture. The kind of thing (NPC, monster, item) sits on top, then the name, then the details. Short values such as challenge rating, spell level, rarity and alignment sit in a small tab on the card.
- **Lists open as rows**, one tidy sheet with column headings, instead of a grid of cards. You can still switch a list back to the grid yourself, and your choice is kept.
- **Sheets** lose the portrait column. What sat beneath it moves into a line of facts under the title, and the text takes the full width.
- If a record has a picture of its own, a dashed button on the sheet lets you open it when you want to look. The picture is only fetched then.
- **On a phone**, rows and cards shrink to a few lines of text with no picture tile.
- While a list loads, it shows grey placeholder lines shaped like text, not like portraits.

## Turn them on or off

1. Open your **Account** page, or the player **Settings** page.
2. Find **Pictures**.
3. Flip **Show AI labels** or **Low art**.

The change applies straight away. Flip the switch again to go back.
