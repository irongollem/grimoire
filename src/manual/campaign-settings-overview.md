Campaign Settings (`/campaign/settings`) is where configuration that applies to the whole campaign — rather than to any one entity — lives, organized into tabs.

## Tabs

- **Details** — the campaign's name and core info.
- **Members & Invites** — who's in the campaign, and shareable invite links to bring in new players.
- **Scheduling** — session scheduling settings.
- **Rules** — toggles for optional systems, including turning **The Interlude** (downtime) on or off for this campaign.
- **Classes / Species** — which custom classes and species are available to players building characters here.
- **AI Assistant / AI Connections** — your AI provider setup (platform credits vs. bring-your-own-key) for generation across the app.
- **Spotify** — connect a Spotify account for the Soundboard.
- **Backup** — export/restore points for your campaign data.
- **World Bundle** — package and share campaign content as a portable bundle.
- **Danger Zone** — irreversible actions, including deleting the campaign.

→ _See The Interlude — Downtime_ for what the Rules tab's downtime toggle controls, and _Soundboard_ for what the Spotify connection is used for.
