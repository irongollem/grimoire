The Character Sheet view (`/character-sheet/:partyMemberId`) generates a printable PDF sheet from a party member's live record — stats, inventory, and all — for the table or for sharing with a player.

## Exporting

Choose a **page size** (A4 or Letter) and a visual **theme**, then click **Export PDF**. The preview on the page reflects your selections before you commit to generating the file.

Because the sheet pulls straight from the party member's current data, it always reflects the latest HP, inventory, and stats — there's nothing to keep in sync separately.

→ _See Party Tracker_ for where the underlying character data is edited.
