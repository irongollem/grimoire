Packs is the last section of the Party page. It shows every pack in the campaign side by side, so you can see who carries what without opening each character in turn.

## What you see

The first column is the **Party stash**, the things nobody carries. After it comes one column for each character in the party, in the party's order. Up to six columns share the width. With more, the section scrolls sideways. On a phone the columns stack as collapsible packs: the stash starts open and the others closed.

**The head of each column** shows the portrait, the name and one line such as "47.5 / 120 lb · ✦ 2/3 · 41 gp": carried weight over capacity, attuned items out of three, and gold. Press the head to open that character's Inventory tab. The stash head reads "carried by nobody" and how many things it holds.

**The body** lists the things under small headings, in the character's own order: Worn, Belt, Backpack, each container by name, and Stored elsewhere. A row shows a rarity dot, the name, a count when there is more than one, and a ✦ when the item is attuned. Items the party does not know about yet (unidentified, or cursed and not revealed) carry a dashed "DM" mark. You always see the true name. An empty column reads "Nothing in the stash." or "Carrying nothing."

## Finding an item

Type in **Find in any pack…** to search item names and notes across every pack, ignoring capital letters. Under it are three chips that narrow the search further:

- **Attuned**: items a character is attuned to.
- **Magic**: items above common rarity.
- **Cursed**: items that are cursed, including ones the party has not discovered are cursed.

While a search or a chip is active:

- A strip at the top lists one line per name with the total and where each one is, so "who has the rope?" is answered at once.
- Columns keep their places. A pack with no match fades and says how many things are hidden. A pack with matches shows them plus "N more hidden".

**Clear** removes the search and the chips.

## Give to

Open the menu on a row (**Actions for** the item) and choose **Give to**. It lists the stash and every character except the one carrying it. For a stack, it asks how many to give.

The item lands in the receiver's backpack, not worn and not attuned. If it is a container, what is inside goes with it. On a desktop you can also drag a row onto another column to give it whole. Dragging is turned off on phones.

The same menu has:

- **One fewer** and **One more**, to change the count,
- **Attune** or **Unattune**,
- **Drop in chat**, to post it to the campaign chat,
- **Open in** the carrier's pack, to jump to their inventory,
- **Make catalogue item**, for a custom item you want to keep as a catalogue entry,
- **Remove**.

Players can give things from their own Inventory too, but can change only their own pack and the stash.

## Adding an item

**Add item** at the top of the section opens a form. Pick an item from the Vault by search or type a custom name, then set the quantity and notes, and choose **Who gets it**: the stash first, then each character. You can also **Drop in chat** or follow the link to create the item in the Vault.

## Character inventories

A character's own Inventory tab, reached by pressing a column head, shows the paper doll, purse, carry weight, belt, backpack and containers, and where you can identify an item or reveal a curse. The stash is not repeated there.

Packs update by themselves when anyone changes an inventory or gold, so you do not need to refresh.
