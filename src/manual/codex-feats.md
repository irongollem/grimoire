The **Feats** tab (**Character Codex → Feats**) lists every feat a character can take. A feat is something a character *chooses*, usually in place of an Ability Score Improvement, while an ability is something a class grants. Abilities have their own tab, see [Abilities Compendium](#abilities-compendium).

## Official feats

The feats of the open-licensed books come with the app and are the same for every account. You can read them, and each one names the book it comes from, but you cannot change or delete them. To change one, make your own feat. Only the app's administrator can edit an official feat.

## Finding a feat

The list can be filtered by:

- **Text search**: matches the name, the prerequisite text and tags.
- **Category**: Origin, General, Fighting Style or Epic Boon. These exist only in the 2024 rules; 2014 feats have no category.
- **Edition**: 2014 or 2024.

The filters persist while you move around, and **Clear** resets them.

## Creating a feat

Click **New Feat**. It is the same editor as an ability, with a **Feat** section added:

- **Category** (2024 rules only).
- **May be taken more than once**.
- **Prerequisites**: the conditions the app checks when a character picks the feat. Every condition you set has to hold: a minimum character level, a minimum ability score (set several to mean "any one of these", like Strength or Dexterity 13), being able to cast a spell, training with an armor type, or having the Fighting Style feature. The free-text **Prerequisite (as written)** field above is the book's wording and is only shown, never checked.
- **Ability score increase**: which abilities the feat raises, by how much, the score it cannot raise past, and whether it may instead be split as +1 to two abilities.

The **Mechanics** section works exactly as it does for an ability (uses, riders, choices and so on), see [Abilities Compendium](#abilities-compendium).

Click **Create** to save the new feat. An existing feat saves itself as you type, and **Done** takes you back to reading it. If a condition or a part of the mechanics is incomplete, a red list says what to fix and autosave waits until it is fixed.
