A notice board is a wall your players can read: a tavern's cork board, an outpost's plank wall. You pin notices to it, and a notice can carry a quest and a handout, so a player picking one up at the table is how the party finds the job. Find boards under **Compendium → Dungeon Craft**, the **Notice Boards** tab.

## Key ideas

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Board | One wall of notices. It has a name and a look (cork or planks), and hangs at one or more places in your Atlas |
| Notice | One thing pinned to the board: a note, a wanted poster, a sealed letter, or a picture |
| Draft | A notice you are still writing. Drafts are not on the board, and players never see them |
| Offer | The quest and the handout a notice gives the party when it is taken |
| Shown to players | Players can open the board from their portal and read it |
| Present now | The board opens on every player's screen, and notices can be taken from it |

## Making a board

Click **New Notice Board**. Give it a **name** and choose its surface:

- **Cork, oak frame**: for a city, an inn, a guildhall.
- **Planks, pine frame**: for an outpost, a camp, a village green.

The board is saved as you type. Click **Done** when you are finished to return to the list.

## Adding notices

Under **Add to the board**, pick what kind of notice to make:

- **Note**: a headline and a few lines, set in the DM's hand.
- **Wanted poster**: a name, the charge, a reward, and a portrait. Use **From an NPC or monster** to take the name and portrait from someone in your campaign, or upload a picture of your own.
- **Sealed letter**: an address on the outside and a letter inside. Players see the sealed letter on the board and read the body when they open it. Pick a red or black wax seal.
- **Picture**: a caption, an image, and words beside it that players read up close.

Each notice has a **Size** (small, medium, large) and shows a live preview of how a player will see it. Everything on a notice saves as you type.

A new notice starts as a **draft**. When it reads the way you want, click **Put it up** to hang it on the board. **Take down** sends it back to drafts. **Delete** removes it for good, along with any picture you uploaded for it.

## Arranging the board

Drag any notice on the board to where you want it. The list of notices beside the board sets their reading order, the order players meet them in when they read the board as a list. Use the arrows to move a notice earlier or later.

## What a notice can offer

Under **Offers**, a notice can carry a **Quest** and a **Handout**. When a player takes the notice:

- the quest is revealed to the whole party, and
- the handout is shared with the whole party.

Leave both empty and the notice is just something to read. Nothing it reveals is undone later, even if you pin the notice back up.

## Showing the board and presenting it

The **Hidden from players** and **Shown to players** switch decides whether players can open the board from their portal. A hidden board does not exist for them.

**Present now** is for the table. It opens the board on every player's screen at once, like walking up to it together, and it is the only time notices can be taken. Players can always read a shown board, but they can only **Take this notice** while you are presenting. That is deliberate: taking a notice reveals a quest to everyone, so it happens when you decide the table is at the board. Click **Stop presenting** to close it on every screen. Players can still read it from the portal.

Only one board per campaign is presented at a time. Presenting another takes the first one down. Hiding a board also stops presenting it.

## When a notice is taken

The first player to take a notice gets it, and everyone else is told who got there first. The notice leaves the board and a stub is left behind naming who took it and in which session. The quest and the handout reach the whole party at that moment.

To hang a taken notice again, open it and click **Pin back up**. The stub goes away and the notice can be taken again. Pinning it back up does not take back the quest or the handout, which stay revealed.

## Hanging a board at a place

At the bottom of the board's page, under **Hangs at**, choose a location and click **Place here**. A board can hang at several places. Players see a place's shown boards in the place's panel, each with a small preview, and a board you are presenting is marked.

## Tips

- Draft a whole wall before the session and put the notices up one at a time as the party explores.
- Present the board when the party walks up to it, not before. The surprise of the screen opening is part of the scene.
- A wanted poster with a reward and a quest attached is a ready-made bounty. Take the name and portrait from the NPC so the face matches the one the players will meet.
