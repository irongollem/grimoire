The Encounter Map (`/encounters/:id/map`) is the battle-map view for a live encounter, reached from **Back to Runner** in the Encounter Runner's top bar and back again the same way.

## Fog of War

The fog toolbox controls what's currently revealed to players:

- **Tool** — switch between painting modes (reveal, hide, pan) to control visibility.
- **Brush shape & size** — shape and scale the area affected by each stroke, for anything from a torchlit room to a single corridor tile.

Changes to the fog are visible to players in real time, so painting during play immediately updates what they can see of the map.

→ _See Encounter Runner_ for initiative, HP, and turn tracking during the same combat.
