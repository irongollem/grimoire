The Gallery (`/gallery`) collects every image you've generated or uploaded across the whole app — NPC portraits, monster art, scenes, maps, item art, and more — automatically, with no extra step on your part.

## Browsing

Filter by kind using the tabs at the top (each shows a count), or search by the prompt used to generate the image. Click **Open source** on any image to jump straight to the NPC, monster, or other entity it belongs to.

## Removing an image

**Remove from gallery** only removes the gallery record — it does not delete the art from the entity it's attached to. There is currently no way to delete art itself from the Gallery; do that from the entity's own editor.
