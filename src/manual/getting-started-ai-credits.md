This page explains what AI generation costs, how to switch it on or off, and what to do when your credits run low. Grimoire can generate NPCs, monsters, items, spells, quests, puzzles, and more with AI, plus narrative and scene art for the Chronicler. **Every plan can use it.** Turn the campaign's AI Assistant on and every **Generate with AI** button you'll find around the app runs for real, billed to credits (Free the same as Pro). Each one shows its credit cost before you confirm, and offers a **Buy credits** link if your balance is short instead of failing outright. Where AI is switched on, and where the Pro extra (the image choice) lives, is **Campaign Settings → AI Assistant**. What the AI writes and paints follows your campaign's Premise and tone, set in the **Canon**.

## Key ideas

| Term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **AI Assistant toggle** | A per-campaign on/off switch, available on every plan. Turning it off hides every AI generation button across the campaign: useful if your table wants a fully hand-crafted experience. |
| **Credits** | Grimoire's built-in currency for AI generation. Every AI request is paid for in credits. Buy packs on any plan (they never expire) and Pro also gets a monthly allowance on top. |

A complimentary beta-tester account (occasionally handed out by the maintainer) counts as Pro everywhere in this page.

## Turning AI on

1. Open **Campaign Settings → AI Assistant**.
2. Flip the **AI Assistant** toggle on. The first time any account does this, a consent notice (required under the EU AI Act) appears: read and confirm it. After that, toggling on/off for any campaign is immediate.
3. The image choice, usage stats, and the Chronicler promotion opt-in are Pro only, and only appear once the toggle is on. On Free, that part of the tab shows a message that those extras are Pro; generation itself still works from the toggle above.

The toggle itself is visible on every plan, and controls generation on every plan: it's only the section beneath it that stays Pro-only.

## Credits

Credits are Grimoire's built-in currency for AI generation.

- **Every plan** generates with AI on credits, once the AI Assistant toggle is on: buy packs from the Billing page as you need them (see [Billing & Subscription](#billing-subscription)).
- **Pro plan** accounts also get a monthly credit allowance on top of any purchased packs.

Your balance and the credit packs available to purchase are on the **Billing** page: open your account menu (click your name at the bottom of the sidebar) and choose **Billing**. Every generate button shows its own cost (a credits badge next to it) before you confirm, so this manual won't quote a number that could drift out of date; costs come from a live pricing table, not a hardcoded constant.

## When you run out of credits

You never have to leave what you're doing to top up. Click any **Generate** button when your balance is too low (or the **Get credits** link next to the red cost) and a **Not enough credits** window opens right there:

1. It tells you what the generation costs and what your balance is.
2. **On Free**, it first suggests **Pro**, which includes a batch of credits every month and removes the Free limits. Tick the withdrawal-right checkbox and click the **Subscribe** button with the monthly price (or the yearly link below it).
3. **Buy a credit pack**: tick its checkbox and click a pack. On Pro this is the only option shown.
4. Checkout opens in Stripe. When you've paid, it brings you straight back to the page you were on, with a confirmation message. Your generator's settings are not kept across checkout, so you may need to fill them in again.

Or close it with **Maybe later**. If you've reached a Free-plan limit (say, you already have 10 NPCs), you'll see the plan-limit message instead: there's no point buying credits for something the plan won't let you save.

## Choosing how images are made

On the **AI Assistant** tab, Pro campaigns get a **Generation** section once AI is on. It asks only what is yours to decide:

- **Images**: choose **Quick** or **Detailed** for portraits and artwork. The approximate credit cost per image is shown beneath.

Grimoire picks the model that writes NPCs, monsters, items, spells and the rest. You don't need to choose or enter anything for text.

Free plans generate with AI too. On Free, the tab shows a Pro-upgrade message in place of this section, whatever the toggle is set to.

## Other things on the AI Assistant tab

Pro only, and only once the **AI Assistant** toggle is on:

- **Your AI Usage**: your total generations and credits spent, shown for the campaign.
- **Chronicler Promotion**: an opt-in checkbox letting Grimoire feature your campaign's AI-generated Chronicler scene illustrations in its own gallery or marketing. Off by default; your campaign name, notes, and player data are never shared either way.

## Other places that spend credits

- **The Familiar** gives every account five free questions a day, and after that an answer costs what it used. Anything it makes for you is quoted first and costs nothing until you confirm. See [Ask your Familiar](#ask-your-familiar).
- **Importing an adventure** with AI shows an estimate before it starts. See [Importing an Adventure](#importing-an-adventure).

## What your players see

Nothing here: AI configuration is entirely DM-only. AI features are not available at all on a young player's account, even at a table where you have switched AI on: see [Young Players' Accounts](#young-players-accounts). Players see the *results* (an NPC's portrait, a generated item) exactly like any other campaign content, with no indication of how it was paid for.

## Tips

> If the **Images** line reads "Image generation isn't available right now," no image option is currently offered. Try again later.

- Turning the **AI Assistant** toggle off is the fastest way to run a fully hand-authored campaign without generation buttons cluttering every screen, on any plan. Turn it back on and every **Generate with AI** button works again, billed to credits.
