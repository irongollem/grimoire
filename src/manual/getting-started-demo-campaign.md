Grimoire is easier to understand with something in it. The demo campaign is a ready-made campaign you can load into your own account: **Sugarwell**, a side-trip from the campaign *Late in the Kind Country*, set up with NPCs, a quest with its story flow, a mapped site, and music on the soundboard. It's a real campaign in your account, not a read-only preview. Open anything, change anything, run a session in it.

## Key ideas

| Term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Demo campaign** | Your own copy of the published demo. It's marked **Demo** in the campaign switcher. |
| **Free of your plan's limits** | The demo and everything that came with it don't count toward your plan's campaign, NPC, location or other limits, so loading it never uses up your one free campaign. Anything **you** add to it does count. |
| **Reset** | Replaces your copy with a fresh one: everything you changed or added in the demo is lost. |
| **Newer version** | The demo is occasionally improved. Your copy never changes underneath you; when a newer version exists, the demo's settings say so, and resetting picks it up. |

## Loading the demo

You can load it from any of these:

- **The first screen** a new DM sees ("Start your campaign"): click **Explore the demo**.
- **The campaign switcher** at the top of the sidebar: **Load demo campaign**. This works even when your plan's campaign limit is reached, because the demo doesn't count.
- **New Campaign**: **Load the demo campaign instead**, in the dialog's footer.

You have one demo at a time. Once it's loaded, these offers disappear until you remove it.

## Resetting or removing it

- **Reset**: open **Campaign Settings → Details** while the demo is your active campaign and click **Reset demo**.
- **Remove**: **Campaign Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Campaign**. Unlike deleting one of your own campaigns, this removes everything in the demo, including anything you added to it. You can load a fresh copy afterwards.

## What your players see

Nothing, unless you invite them. The demo arrives with a pre-made party rather than real players, and it has no members besides you. If you want to try the player portal, invite a player to the demo the same way as to any campaign.

## Tips

- The demo asks whether to turn on AI assistance, just like a campaign you create yourself. That choice is always yours; loading the demo never turns AI on.
- Want to keep something you built in the demo? Copy it to one of your own campaigns with **Copy to campaign…** before you reset or remove it.
