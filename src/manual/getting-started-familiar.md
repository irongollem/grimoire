The Familiar is Grimoire's assistant. You type what you want in plain words, and it reads your campaign, answers, lays out ideas, or offers to make something for you. It is in **Beta**, so it will sometimes get things wrong. Check anything that matters before the table sees it.

It is there for the DM, in campaigns with AI switched on.

## Where to find it

- The **Ask your Familiar** button sits beside search: in the sidebar on a desktop, in the top bar on a tablet or phone. A small dot on it means a result you have not seen yet is waiting.
- Press **Cmd+J** (Mac) or **Ctrl+J** (Windows and Linux) from any DM screen. Cmd+K or Ctrl+K stays search.
- In search, type two or more characters and the first row of the results reads **Ask your Familiar**, with your words under it. Pick it, or press Cmd/Ctrl+Enter, and the Familiar opens and gets your question. Plain Enter still searches as it always did.

On a desktop the Familiar opens as a column beside the page. On a phone it is a sheet above the bottom bar. It knows which page you are on and which record you have open, so you can say "give her a rival" without naming her. **New conversation** starts fresh.

## What to ask

- **Your campaign.** "Who runs the harbour guild?", "What did the party learn about the lighthouse?", "Which quests mention the Ashen Court?" It reads your records and tells you which ones it read: use **Show what I read** to see them.
- **Ideas.** Ask for ideas and each one is marked **Canon** (it follows what is already in your world) or **New idea**. An idea can hand off to a generator when you like it.
- **The rules.** Ask how grappling works or what a spell does. It answers from the rules books your campaign has switched on, in your campaign's edition. When none of those books cover it, it says so and marks anything it adds from general knowledge as not from your books.
- **Making things.** "I need a new NPC, a half-elf about 27." See the next section.
- **How Grimoire works.** "What does *known to* mean on a connection?" It looks the answer up in this manual rather than guessing. If the manual does not cover your question, it tells you and notes the gap so the manual can grow.

It stays on your campaign and on Grimoire. A request that has nothing to do with either gets a short refusal and costs nothing.

## Making things

When you ask for something a generator can make (an NPC, a monster, an item, a place, an encounter and many more), the Familiar asks only what it must, then shows a **quote card**:

- a line for each thing it understood, such as the kind of person and the age,
- a line for each tie to something already in your campaign,
- a **Total** and a button that reads like **Create NPC · 3 credits**.

Nothing is made and nothing is spent until you press that button. You can:

- **Adjust** the generator's choices (toggles, chips and fields) before you confirm,
- **Cancel**: "Cancelled. Nothing was spent."

### Yes or no on ties to existing records

If the new thing would be tied to a record that already exists (a parent, a home, a faction, a place for an encounter), that line has a checkbox. A tie you named yourself starts ticked. A tie the Familiar suggests on its own starts unticked. Tick or untick each one: a line left unticked is written as if you had never mentioned it.

When you ask for a parent, sibling or spouse of someone, the Familiar also offers the rest of that family (for example the person's other children). Those start unticked, because half-siblings exist.

### It does not hold up the chat

After you confirm, the quote is replaced by a waiting block and you can keep asking questions. When the result is ready it lands at the bottom of the conversation, and the waiting block turns into **See it below**. If you closed the Familiar or moved on, a small notice tells you: **Stay here** or **Open** the new record. A result card shows each part with a tick or a cross. A part that failed (a picture, for example) can be retried on its own with its price shown, and the record itself stays saved.

Some generators work in their own screen. For those the Familiar fills in your request and opens the screen, and you press that screen's own button. Nothing is quoted in the chat.

## Free questions and credits

Every account gets **five free questions a day**. The line under the box shows how many are left, for example "4 of 5 free questions left today". After the fifth, an answer costs what it used, charged in whole credits. If you cannot cover the next answer, the Familiar says "That was today's five free questions." and offers **Get credits** and **Upgrade to Pro**. The free five come back tomorrow.

Asking the Familiar to make something is different: the question that leads to a quote is free, and you pay the generator's price only when you press the quote's button. Report drafts are free too.

## Reporting a bug or asking for a feature

Tell the Familiar that something is broken, or that you wish Grimoire did something. It writes the report for you, using the page you are on, and opens the report form already filled in with a **Review and send…** button.

Read it, change what you like, and press **Send** in the form. **Nothing is sent until you press Send in the form.** The Familiar never sends a report by itself. If it cannot work out a draft, it puts your own words in the form for you to finish.

## What it will not do

- It does not make things without a quote and your press of the button.
- It does not answer questions that are not about your campaign or Grimoire.
- It does not work inside an open editor (for example it will not rewrite the text you are editing).
- Some generators it cannot run from the chat yet. It says "I can't run that one from here yet." and points you to the page.
