Grimoire's controls are the same everywhere, but they're laid out differently depending on your device. This page covers the parts that aren't specific to any one feature: the sidebar, the campaign switcher, search, keyboard shortcuts, and how phone/tablet layouts differ from desktop.

## Key ideas

| Term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Sidebar / bottom bar** | The desktop nav is a sidebar; a touch device gets a bottom bar instead. Which one you see depends on your **pointer type**, not your screen width: a touchscreen laptop gets the bar even at a wide window, and a mouse-driven tablet gets the sidebar. |
| **Prep / Play mode** | The bottom bar's set of tabs changes automatically depending on whether your campaign session is currently running: Prep while you're building, Play once you've started the table. |
| **The More sheet** | On a touch device, everything that doesn't fit the bottom bar lives behind the **More** tab, as "All sections." |

## Using the sidebar (desktop)

The sidebar, always visible on a wide pointer-driven window, has, top to bottom:

1. **Grimoire** logo (click to go to the Dashboard) and the dice roller.
2. The **session rail** (DM only): shows and controls the campaign session.
3. The **campaign switcher**: see below.
4. **Global search**: see below.
5. The three nav groups (Campaign / Compendium / Publish): see [Welcome to Grimoire](#welcome-to-grimoire).
6. Your account menu, at the bottom: click your name to open it.

The account menu holds: **Edit display name**, the DM/Player mode toggle, **Account**, **Billing** (DM only, shows a gold "Pro" badge if you're subscribed), **Install app** (on supporting browsers), **Report a bug**, and **Sign out**.

## Switching campaigns

Click the campaign name at the top of the sidebar to open the switcher. It lists every campaign you DM, and lets you:

- Select any campaign to make it active.
- Open its **Archived** section to see campaigns you've archived, with a **Restore** action (upgrade-gated if you're at your campaign limit).
- **Claim unclaimed data**: pulls in any notes, NPCs, or other rows created before you had a campaign to put them in.
- **New Campaign**: see [Creating Your First Campaign](#creating-your-first-campaign).
- **Import from backup**: restore a campaign from a Grimoire backup file.

A green dot next to the campaign name shows how many other members are currently online in it.

## Searching the campaign

Click the search box (or press **⌘K** / **Ctrl+K** from anywhere, it works even while you're typing in another field) to jump straight to an NPC, a Bestiary entry, a Note, a Spell, an Item, a Location, or a Quest by name. Type at least two characters; results are grouped by type, and you can move between them with the arrow keys and open one with Enter.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Press **?** anywhere to open the **Keyboard shortcuts** cheat sheet: it lists exactly what's bound on the screen you're on, grouped into "On this screen" and "Anywhere," so it's never out of date with what this page says.

Two shortcuts work from anywhere in the app:

| Shortcut | Does |
| --- | --- |
| **⌘K** / **Ctrl+K** | Focus the search box (see above). |
| **⌘⇧K** / **Ctrl+Shift+K** | Open the sound palette to fire a sound or scene from wherever you are, without leaving the screen you're on. |

Shortcuts never fire while you're typing in a text field, with the one deliberate exception above: it's meant to pull focus away from whatever you were typing.

## Phone and tablet layout

On a touch device, the sidebar is replaced by a bottom bar:

- The centre button is a gold **+** (Prep mode) to create something in the section you're viewing, or the dice roller (Play mode).
- A handful of tabs sit either side of it (the busiest sections for whichever mode you're in), ranked by how often you'd reach for them, not by the sidebar's grouping. More tabs appear as the window gets wider.
- **More** opens the "All sections" sheet: the campaign switcher, a context-aware create button, the session rail, and every nav item as a grid of tiles, grouped the same way the sidebar is. A small gold dot marks which tiles are already pinned to the bar.

You don't choose Prep or Play directly: the bar switches automatically based on whether your campaign session is running (see [Running a Session](#running-a-session)).

## Getting help on any screen

Most screens carry a small **ⓘ** icon next to their title or near a specific control. Clicking it opens this manual straight to the page that explains that screen: the same manual you're reading now, at **Compendium → Reliquary → Manual**.

## What your players see

Players get their own bottom navigation and their own account menu (with the DM/Player mode toggle, so a player can switch to DM mode without a second account): the sidebar's Campaign/Compendium/Publish groups are DM-only. See [Player Portal: Overview](#player-portal-overview) for the player-side layout.

## Tips

> If a nav item you expect is missing, check **Campaign Settings → Rules**: Workshop and Interlude only appear once their optional rule is switched on for the active campaign.

- A brand-new account gets a one-time guided tour (skippable, and it won't come back once finished) pointing out the party list, quests, the session clock, the sidebar, and the account menu.
- Everything in Compendium is visible with no campaign selected; everything in Campaign is dimmed until you pick or create one.
