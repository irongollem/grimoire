A campaign is the container for everything you build in Grimoire — notes, NPCs, quests, maps, monsters, and your players' characters. Create one from the campaign switcher at the top of the sidebar (or, on a phone, inside the **More** sheet).

## Key ideas

| Term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Setting** | A free-text label (Forgotten Realms, Eberron, your own homebrew…) that drives which calendar and "Populate from Setting" content is offered. |
| **Ruleset** | 2014 or 2024 D&D 5e rules. Chosen at creation and changeable later from **Campaign Settings → Rules**; it governs character options, spells, and creature stat blocks. |
| **Calendar** | The in-world date system used by the Calendar and travel features. |
| **Optional rules** | Toggles for house-rule-style mechanics (turn timers, crafting, encumbrance, and more) — off by default, switched on per campaign in **Campaign Settings → Rules**. |

## Creating a campaign

1. Open the campaign switcher (top of the sidebar) and click **New Campaign**.
2. Enter a **Name**.
3. Enter a **World** — type freely, or pick a suggestion (Forgotten Realms, Eberron, Ravenloft, Dragonlance, Greyhawk, Planescape, Spelljammer, Dark Sun, Mystara, Homebrew).
4. Choose a **Ruleset** — D&D 5e (2014) or D&D 5e (2024). This applies to character options, spells, creatures, items, rests, and encounter rules.
5. Choose a **Calendar** and the **Current Year**. Picking **— Custom calendar…** opens a calendar editor so you can define your own months and days.
6. If this is your very first campaign, an **Import existing data** checkbox appears — leave it checked to assign any notes, NPCs, party members, calendar events, and encounters you already created (before you had a campaign to put them in) to this one.
7. Click **Create Campaign**.

## Campaign Settings overview

Open **Campaign Settings** (the gear in the sidebar's Campaign group, or the settings icon next to any campaign in the switcher) to configure every aspect of your campaign. It's a DM-only screen, split into tabs:

| Tab | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| **Details** | Name, world, calendar/year, theme, health visibility, immersive rolls, VTT token visibility, and Populate from Setting. |
| **Members & Invites** | The member list and invite-link management — see [Inviting Players](#inviting-players). |
| **Scheduling** | Session scheduling and player availability. |
| **Rules** | Ruleset (2014/2024) and every optional rule toggle, including the ones that unlock Workshop and Interlude in the sidebar. |
| **Classes** | Campaign-specific custom class/archetype settings. |
| **Species** | Campaign-specific species settings. |
| **AI Assistant** | AI generation on/off, available on every plan; your own key instead of credits is Pro only. See [AI Generation & Credits](#ai-generation-credits). |
| **AI Connections** | Connects an external AI assistant to your campaign over MCP. |
| **Spotify** | Spotify integration for ambient music. |
| **Backup** | Campaign backup/export. |
| **World Bundle** | Import a `.grimoire` world bundle exported from another campaign. |
| **Import Document** | Import a PDF, page photos, or pasted text into game content — see [Importing an Adventure](#importing-an-adventure). |
| **Danger Zone** | Transfer ownership or delete the campaign. |

## Setting up the Details tab

The essentials live on the **Details** tab:

- **Name**, **World**, **Calendar**, and **Current Year** — same fields as at creation; edit any of them here later.
- **Populate from Setting** — appears once your World matches a recognised setting. Seeds locations, notable NPCs, and factions for that setting in one click; safe to run more than once, as it skips anything you already have.
- **Theme** — the app's colour palette for this campaign.
- **Health Visibility** — how HP appears to players: **Strategic** (bars + labels for everyone, exact numbers for player characters only), **Immersive** (player characters show a bar with no numbers; monsters show status words only), or **Unknown** (no health info shown for anyone but player characters).
- **Immersive Rolls** — stealth, knowledge, and insight checks show only flavor text in chat; the full result is whispered to you, the DM, only.
- **Show VTT tokens to players** — turn this off for in-person sessions using physical minis or theater of the mind; players' battle map then shows only the map and fog of war, with no tokens. Your own view is unaffected.

## After creating the campaign

Your next steps, roughly in order:

1. Go to **Atlas** and click **Populate from Setting** to seed your world's major locations — and, if your campaign involves planar travel, **Populate Planes** on the same screen to seed all 21 D&D cosmological planes.
2. Go to **Factions** and click **Populate from Setting** to seed relevant organisations.
3. Go to **Campaign Settings → Members & Invites** and generate links for your players.
4. Open **Campaign Settings → Rules** to turn on any optional rules you want (2014/2024 ruleset, crafting, the Interlude, and a dozen others) — this also decides whether Workshop and Interlude appear in your sidebar.
5. Have an adventure already written up? **Campaign Settings → Import Document** turns a PDF, photos, or pasted text into NPCs, monsters, locations, items, and a quest in one pass — see [Importing an Adventure](#importing-an-adventure). Otherwise, create your first NPCs, quests, and encounters by hand.

## What your players see

Nothing yet — a campaign has no players until you invite them. See [Inviting Players](#inviting-players).

## Tips

- Free-plan accounts are limited to a small number of campaigns; the app shows an upgrade prompt if you hit that limit when creating a new one. Check the Billing page (account menu → **Billing**) for your exact quota.
- The ruleset choice (2014/2024) can be changed later from **Campaign Settings → Rules**, but changing it can alter character progression and available content — existing character choices are preserved for your review rather than silently discarded.
