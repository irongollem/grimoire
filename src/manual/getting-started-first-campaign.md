A campaign is the container for everything you build in Grimoire: notes, NPCs, quests, maps, monsters, and your players' characters. This page is for the first ten minutes: making the campaign, choosing what it plays by, and knowing where to go next. Create one from the campaign switcher at the top of the sidebar (or, on a phone, inside the **More** sheet).

## Key ideas

| Term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Setting** | A free-text label (Forgotten Realms, Eberron, your own homebrew…) that drives which calendar and "Populate from Setting" content is offered. |
| **Game system** | **D&D 5e (2014)**, **D&D 5e (2024)** or **Generic**. It governs character options, spells, creature stat blocks and which screens appear. Chosen at creation; the two 5e editions can be switched later from **Campaign Settings → Rules**, Generic cannot. |
| **Calendar** | The in-world date system used by the Calendar and travel features. |
| **Optional rules** | Toggles for house-rule-style mechanics (turn timers, crafting, encumbrance, and more): off by default, switched on per campaign in **Campaign Settings → Rules**. |

## Creating a campaign

1. Open the campaign switcher (top of the sidebar) and click **New Campaign**.
2. Pick the **GAME SYSTEM**: **D&D 5e (2014)**, **D&D 5e (2024)** or **Generic**. For a 5e system, an **Allow characters built for…** checkbox lets characters of the other edition join as they are. Generic hides every rules-system screen and is for story-and-world tables: see [Campaign Settings](#campaign-settings).
3. Enter a **Name**.
4. Enter a **World**: type freely, or pick a suggestion (Forgotten Realms, Eberron, Ravenloft, Dragonlance, Greyhawk, Planescape, Spelljammer, Dark Sun, Mystara, Homebrew).
5. Choose a **Calendar** and the **Current Year**. Picking **Blank calendar (make your own)…** opens a calendar editor so you can define your own months and days.
6. If this is your very first campaign, an **Import existing data** checkbox appears: leave it checked to assign any notes, NPCs, party members, calendar events, and encounters you already created (before you had a campaign to put them in) to this one.
7. Click **Create Campaign**.

## Campaign Settings overview

Open **Campaign Settings** (the gear in the sidebar's Campaign group, or the settings icon next to any campaign in the switcher) to configure every aspect of your campaign. It's a DM-only screen, split into tabs:

| Tab | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| **Details** | Name, theme, health visibility, map memory, sight, immersive rolls and VTT token visibility. The calendar, the current date, the climate, the coins and Populate from Setting live in **Canon**, under *How the world works*. |
| **Members & Invites** | The member list and invite-link management: see [Inviting Players](#inviting-players). |
| **Scheduling** | Session scheduling and player availability. |
| **Rules** | The game system (switch between the 5e editions) and every optional rule toggle, including the ones that unlock Workshop and Interlude in the sidebar. |
| **Travel** | How fast the party moves over each kind of terrain and by each way of travelling. |
| **Classes** | Which classes your players can pick. |
| **Species** | Which species your players can pick. |
| **AI Assistant** | AI generation on/off, available on every plan, paid in credits. See [AI Generation & Credits](#ai-generation-credits). Owner only. |
| **AI Connections** | Connects an external AI assistant to your campaign over MCP. |
| **Spotify** | Spotify integration for ambient music. |
| **Backup** | Campaign backup/export. |
| **World Bundle** | Import a world bundle exported from another campaign. |
| **Open Book** | Share a read-only copy of the campaign's story with anyone who has the link. Owner only: see [Open Book](#open-book-your-campaign-as-a-public-book). |
| **Danger Zone** | Transfer ownership or delete the campaign. Owner only. |

A co-DM sees the tabs except the three marked owner only. **Classes** and **Species** are hidden in a Generic campaign.

## Setting up the Details tab

The essentials live on the **Details** tab:

- **Name**: the same field as at creation. The World, the calendar, today's date, the climate and the coins are on the **Canon** page now, under *Premise and tone* and *How the world works*.
- **Theme**: the app's colour palette for this campaign.
- **Health Visibility**: how HP appears to players: **Strategic** (bars + labels for everyone, exact numbers for player characters only), **Immersive** (player characters show a bar with no numbers; monsters show status words only), or **Unknown** (no health info shown for anyone but player characters).
- **Immersive Rolls**: stealth, knowledge, and insight checks show only flavor text in chat; the full result is whispered to you, the DM, only.
- **Map memory** and **Sight**: whether the map keeps what the party has seen, and whether a player's phone shows what the whole party sees or only their own character. The defaults suit most tables.
- **Show VTT tokens to players**: turn this off for in-person sessions using physical minis or theater of the mind; players' battle map then shows only the map and fog of war, with no tokens. Your own view is unaffected.

## After creating the campaign

Your next steps, roughly in order:

1. Open **Canon** and find *How the world works*. Once your calendar matches a recognised setting, a **Populate from…** panel with a **Populate** button appears: one click seeds that setting's locations, notable NPCs and factions. It is safe to run twice, as it skips anything you already have.
2. If your campaign involves planar travel, open **Atlas** and click **Populate Planes** to seed all 21 D&D cosmological planes.
3. Go to **Campaign Settings → Members & Invites** and generate links for your players.
4. Open **Campaign Settings → Rules** to turn on any optional rules you want (crafting and the Interlude are on by default, plus a dozen others): this also decides whether Workshop and Interlude appear in your sidebar.
5. Have an adventure already written up? **Canon → Bring in a book** turns a PDF, photos, or pasted text into NPCs, monsters, locations, items, and a quest in one pass: see [Importing an Adventure](#importing-an-adventure). Otherwise, create your first NPCs, quests, and encounters by hand.

## What your players see

Nothing yet: a campaign has no players until you invite them. See [Inviting Players](#inviting-players).

## Tips

- Free-plan accounts are limited to a small number of campaigns; the app shows an upgrade prompt if you hit that limit when creating a new one. Check the Billing page (account menu → **Billing**) for your exact quota.
- The edition choice (2014/2024) can be changed later from **Campaign Settings → Rules**, but changing it can alter character progression and available content: existing character choices are preserved for your review rather than silently discarded.
