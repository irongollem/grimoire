## Creating a campaign

From the Dashboard, click **New Campaign**. Give it a name and pick a world setting. The setting choice matters — it drives which factions, locations, and calendar events the "Populate from Setting" buttons will seed.

Available settings include Faerûn, Eberron, Greyhawk, Dragonlance, Ravenloft, Planescape, Spelljammer, Dark Sun, Mystara, and a generic Fantasy option.

## Campaign Settings overview

Open **Campaign Settings** (gear icon on the Dashboard or sidebar) to configure every aspect of your campaign. Settings are split across several tabs:

### Details tab

- **Name** — the campaign's title (shown in page headers and invites).
- **Setting** — your world. Changing this after creation also updates what "Populate from Setting" will seed.
- **Calendar** — which calendar system to use. Faerûn uses the Harptos calendar; other settings have their own. You can pick Gregorian for a real-world feel.
- **Current In-World Year** — used as the default year when adding calendar events.
- **Health Visibility** — controls how HP appears to players:
  - _Strategic_: players see exact numbers for everyone.
  - _Immersive_: players see their own HP numerically; other characters use descriptive labels (Healthy, Hurt, Bloodied, etc.).
  - _Unknown_: no HP information visible to players.
- **Immersive Rolls** — hides raw dice results in chat; players see only narrative outcomes.
- **Multiclass Prerequisites** — enforces or ignores ability score requirements for multiclassing.

### Populate from Setting

The **Populate from Setting** button (Details tab) seeds iconic content for your chosen world: ~70 locations for Faerûn, 15–20 for other settings, plus factions and calendar events. This is safe to run multiple times — it skips content you already have.

The **Populate Planes** button seeds all 21 D&D cosmological planes as a location hierarchy if your campaign involves planar travel.

## After creating the campaign

Your next steps, roughly in order:

1. Go to **Atlas → Locations** and click "Populate from Setting" to seed your world's major locations.
2. Go to **Factions** and click "Populate from Setting" to seed relevant organisations.
3. Go to **Campaign Settings → Invites** and generate links for your players.
4. Import SRD content — monsters, spells, and items each have a one-click import button.
5. Create your first NPCs, quests, and encounters.
