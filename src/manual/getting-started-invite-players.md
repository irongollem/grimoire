## Generating an invite link

Open **Campaign Settings → Invites** and click **New Invite**. You can optionally set:

- **Label** — a DM-only note so you remember which invite you sent to whom (e.g. "Mira's link").
- **Expiry** — a date after which the link no longer works.
- **Max Uses** — the number of players who can use this link before it's deactivated.

Click **Copy Link** to copy the join URL to your clipboard. Send it to your player via any channel (Discord, WhatsApp, email, etc.).

## What players experience

When a player visits their invite link:

1. If they're not signed in, they see a sign-up / sign-in form first.
2. After authenticating, Grimoire automatically adds them to your campaign as a **Player**.
3. They're taken directly to the Player Portal — they never see any DM screens.

Players are permanently linked to your campaign. They can return at any time by logging into Grimoire.

## Assigning characters

After a player joins, go to **Campaign Settings → Members**. Find their row and use the character assignment dropdown to link them to a party member (character sheet). Players can also create their own characters from the portal if you prefer.

## Revoking access

Click the **Revoke** button next to any invite link to deactivate it. To remove a player entirely, use the **Remove** button on their row in the Members tab. This unlinks them from the campaign but doesn't delete their Grimoire account.

## The green presence dot

The Members tab shows a **green dot** next to any player who is currently online in the campaign. Useful during sessions to see who's connected.

## DM Preview Mode

As DM you can browse the full Player Portal to see exactly what your players see. Use the **Preview** button in the sidebar. While in Preview Mode, you can also pass a `?memberId=` query parameter to the portal URL to see the view as a specific character.
