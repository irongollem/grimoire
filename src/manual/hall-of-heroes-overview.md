The Hall of Heroes (`/hall-of-heroes`) is a library of ready-made, iconic characters — organized by setting — that any DM can pull into a campaign as an instant NPC or a quick-start player character.

## Using a hero

Filter by **Setting** to find heroes that match your campaign's world, then click **Add to Campaign** on any card to import it into your active campaign. Heroes whose setting matches your campaign's are sorted to the top automatically.

## Managing the Hall (admin only)

Adding, editing, and deleting heroes — and the **Sync All Settings** action that populates the Hall from source data — are restricted to app admins, since this is a shared library used across every campaign on Grimoire, not per-campaign content.
