The Hall of Heroes (**Compendium → Hall of Heroes** in the sidebar, `/hall-of-heroes`) is a library of ready-made, iconic characters — organised by D&D setting — that any DM can pull into a campaign as an instant NPC. It's shared across every campaign on Grimoire rather than owned by one, the same as the SRD compendium.

## Key ideas

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Hero | A pre-written character with portrait, identity, and lore, tied to one D&D setting |
| Setting | The published world a hero belongs to (Faerûn, Eberron, and others) |
| Add to Campaign | Imports the hero as a new NPC into your active campaign |

## Finding a hero

Use the **search box** (name, species, occupation, tags) and the **Setting** filter to narrow the list. Heroes whose setting matches your active campaign's calendar/setting float to the top automatically and carry a small ✦ marker on their card.

## Using a hero

1. Open a hero's card, or click through to its detail page.
2. Click **Add to Campaign**. This requires an active campaign — the button is disabled and shows "No active campaign" otherwise.
3. The hero is imported as a new NPC into your active campaign's NPC list, and you're taken straight to **NPCs**.

Adding a hero doesn't remove or alter the original — it copies the hero's stat block and lore into a fresh NPC row, so you're free to edit that NPC afterward without touching the shared library entry.

## Browsing a hero's detail page

Each hero's page shows:

- Portrait, species, alignment, occupation, age, and a colour-coded status (alive / dead / missing / unknown).
- Tags.
- **Appearance**, **Personality**, and **Backstory** — rich text, shown to any DM browsing the Hall.
- **DM Notes** — rich text, visible only to app admins.

## Managing the Hall (app admins only)

Adding, editing, and deleting heroes is restricted to app admins, since this is a library shared across every campaign on Grimoire rather than per-campaign content. The editor (**New Hero** / **Edit**) has:

- Portrait with focal-point control.
- **Setting** — picked from the list of D&D settings.
- **Name** (required), **Species**, **Alignment** (the full nine-alignment grid, or Unaligned), **Occupation**, **Age**.
- **Status** — Alive / Dead / Missing / Unknown.
- **Tags**.
- **Appearance**, **Personality**, **Backstory**, **DM Notes** — each a rich-text field.

**Sync All Settings** (also admin-only) bulk-seeds the Hall from its source data across every setting in one operation, reporting how many heroes were added and updated.

## What your players see

The Hall of Heroes itself is DM-only — players never browse it directly. Once you **Add to Campaign**, the resulting NPC follows the normal NPC visibility rules: players see nothing about it until you make it (or specific fields of it) player-visible.

## Tips

- Importing the same hero twice creates two separate NPCs — the Hall doesn't track which heroes you've already used in a given campaign.
- A hero without a portrait shows a plain initial placeholder on its card; add one from the hero's own edit screen if you're an app admin, or from the resulting NPC's own editor after import.
