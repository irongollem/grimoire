# Hall of the Fallen

Some champions do not make it home, and some simply hang up their sword. The Hall of the Fallen keeps them: a candlelit wall with one card for every character who fell or retired, so the table can say goodbye properly.

## Marking a character fallen

Only the DM marks a character fallen, and only on purpose. Three failed death saves never do it by themselves, because revivify, resurrection and a merciful DM all exist.

- **From the character:** open the character's sheet from the Party Tracker, open the **⋯** menu and choose **Mark as fallen…**.
- **From combat:** when a player character fails their third death save, the encounter runner offers **Mark as fallen…** under their row. **Not yet** leaves them be.

The dialog asks for the date in your campaign's calendar (it starts at today's date there), the date at the table, and your account of how it happened. **Struck down by** is optional. While you write, the card shows how they will be remembered.

The character leaves the Party Tracker, the encounter roster and the other active lists. Their sheet stays exactly as it was.

## Retiring a character

A player can retire their own character, and the DM can retire anyone's. Retirement is a choice: a player who does not want their character on the wall simply never retires them. A retired champion is honoured rather than mourned, with a gilt card and a laurel wreath instead of a mourning border.

## Bringing them back

Death and retirement are not always final. On a fallen character's sheet the DM can choose **Restore to life**; on a retired one, the DM or the player can choose **Return to the party**. Everything that was written stays, in case they fall again.

## The wall

Players find it in the player portal under **Hall of the Fallen**; the DM reaches it from the **Hall of the Fallen** button on the Party Tracker. Each card turns over: the front honours who they were, the back reads like an obituary, with your account of how it happened and the player's own last words.

- **Last words:** after a fall the character's player sees a notice the next time they open the portal, and can write their goodbye there or later from the back of the card.
- **Candles:** anyone at the table can light a candle on a fallen companion's card.
- **After leaving a campaign:** your own characters and the companions who fell beside you stay on your wall. You are asked once whether to keep each of your own cards; letting one go only removes it from your wall.
