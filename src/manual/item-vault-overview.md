The **Vault** (`/vault`) is your library of item templates — mundane gear and magic items alike. Everything a character can carry, a shop can sell, or a chest can hold starts here as a template; giving one to an NPC or party member creates an inventory entry that references it.

## Building your library

- **Import SRD Items** — pulls the full SRD 2024 item list into your Vault in one click. Safe to re-run; already-imported items are skipped.
- **Generate** — describe an item concept and Grimoire drafts the full record (type, rarity, description, mechanical text).
- **New Item** — build one by hand from scratch.

## Finding items

Filter by type, rarity, or source (SRD vs. your own homebrew). Toggle **Show items from all campaigns** to search your entire library instead of just the active campaign's scoped items.

## What an item record contains

Type, rarity, weight, value, a rich-text description, and free-form **tags** that drive automation elsewhere in the app — the `container` tag turns an item into a container slot, ammo tags link arrows to bows, and crafting recipes match ingredients by tag rather than exact name.

→ _See Item Tags Overview_ for the full list of tag-driven systems, and _Containers_ / _Ranged Weapons & Ammunition_ for two of them in depth.
