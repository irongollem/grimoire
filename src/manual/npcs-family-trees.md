An NPC's family is kept separate from their other connections. Parents, spouses, siblings and children get their own block on the sheet, and together they draw a family tree.

## The Family block

Open an NPC and go to the **Relations** tab. The **Family** block sits at the top, above **Connections**. It groups relatives under **Parents**, **Spouse**, **Siblings**, **Children** and **Relatives**. Siblings who share a parent are worked out for you ("from shared parents"), so you never add them twice.

Each relative is a card with their name, the relation and a short line of life facts. Click the name to open them. The `...` menu on a card has **Remove link**. To change how someone is related, use the picker on the card's relation word.

If the family has no one yet, the block reads "No family yet."

### Adding a relative

Press **Add relative**. The dialog is called **Add a relative**.

1. Under "<name> is the...", choose the relation: **Parent of**, **Child of**, **Spouse of**, **Former spouse of**, **Sibling of** or **Relative of**.
2. Under **Who**, search your NPCs and characters, or press **+ New NPC "name"** to create the person on the spot.
3. For a parent link, choose **Parent**, **Father** or **Mother**, and tick **Adoptive** if it applies. When the person has a spouse, a checkbox offers to make the child the spouse's too.
4. Add a **Note** if you like, for example "born the winter the river froze". With AI on for the campaign, the dialog also offers **Draft a child with AI** (or parent, spouse or sibling, to match the relation), which opens the [NPC Generator](#npc-generator) with the family tie already filled in.
5. Press the save button. Its label follows the relation: **Add child**, **Add parent**, **Add spouse**, **Add former spouse**, **Add sibling** or **Add relative**.

Grimoire checks the link as you go. It will not let someone become their own ancestor, and it tells you when a link looks the wrong way round and offers to swap it (**Keep it anyway** is there if you meant it).

## The family tree

Press **Open tree** in the Family block. The tree opens on its own page, headed **Family of** and the person's name, with a back button to their sheet. Parents sit above, children below, spouses side by side. On a phone it draws the same tree on smaller cards, and a drag pans it.

The toolbar has three toggles that decide who is drawn:

- **The dead**
- **Married in**
- **PCs**

Press **Clear** to put them back. Player characters are included, so a character's family can sit in the same tree as the NPCs. **Add relative** is also on the tree page, for adding someone without going back.

A house faction has a **Lineage** tab, with the family line, who inherits and the house's seat. See the faction pages for that.

Players see the Family block only when there is family to show, and they cannot change it.

## Sort out family links

Campaigns that recorded relatives before family trees existed have old links that just say "Family". Grimoire cannot tell who is whose parent from that, so on the NPC list and the World web a notice asks "Which way do your families go?" It tells you how many links are waiting.

Press **Sort them out** to open the **Sort Out Family Links** page, or **Later** to hide the notice for now (it comes back next session until the work is done).

On that page every old link is listed as "Linked as "Family"" with a reason ("Why we think so") when Grimoire can guess from birth years or notes. In the column headed "They are...", pick the real relation for each pair with the buttons beside it.

- **Accept all n suggestions** takes every guess at once.
- **Save n** saves the ones you chose.
- **Leave the rest as relatives** keeps what you did not choose as plain **Relative of** links, which you can still change later on either person's sheet.

When every link is sorted, the page says "Every family link is sorted." and the notice goes away. New relatives are added from an NPC sheet with their direction chosen then, so this only needs doing once.

## Tips

- Add the parents first, then the children. Siblings appear by themselves.
- Use a **Note** on a link for details that the tree should not show, such as how an adoption happened.
- Use **Connections**, not Family, for things that are not kin, such as a mentor or a rival.
