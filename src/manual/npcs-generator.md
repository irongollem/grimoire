The NPC Generator makes NPCs faster than the full editor. You fill in a handful of fields yourself, or describe a concept and let AI write the rest. Click the NPC Tracker's **Generate** button, or **Add relative** on a Family block. The Familiar opens on the right with an NPC card. When AI is on, the Family dialog also offers **Draft a child with AI** (or parent, spouse, sibling), which opens the same card with the family tie already filled in. **Quick create** is there with AI off. The AI half appears only when the campaign has AI switched on.

## Key ideas

- **Quick create** works with or without AI turned on. It is always available.
- **Generate** needs your campaign's AI generation switched on (in Campaign Settings) and enough credits to cover the cost. It works the same on every plan.
- Everything you fill in on the card is a constraint the AI is told to respect, not just a starting point it might ignore.

## The card

The first field is **What to make**. Describe the NPC in a sentence or two. Example: "A mysterious tiefling bard who works as a city informant and hides a dark past." Type **@** to include an item from your Vault in the NPC's inventory. The gilt button stays off until you have written something.

Under it you can fill in any of the options: **Name** (drawn from a name table if left blank), **Species**, **Age**, **Alignment** and **Role**. Two folds hold the rest. **Where they stand** has the place, the faction and the role in it, the person they are tied to and how, a second parent, and other ties. **At the table** has their attitude and the stat block template. Two switches finish the card: **Portrait** (on by default) and **Alter ego**.

Picking a faction or a person here creates that membership or connection the moment the NPC is made, so you don't need a second trip to the Relations tab. The relationship list has a **Family** group (parent, child, spouse, sibling and so on) and a **Bond** group (mentor, rival and the like). Choosing a child offers **and of** for an optional second parent, and the card lists other relatives you can link in the same step. Each starts unticked. **Tie it to** adds any other tie, to a god, a place, a faction, an item or a character.

## Quick create

Click the quiet **Quick create** button on the card. It makes the NPC from your options with no AI and no credits. A name is drawn if you left it blank, and what you wrote in **What to make** is saved as the NPC's DM note. It does not give the NPC stats unless you picked a stat block template.

## Generate with AI

The card shows the price before you commit. Click the gilt button to generate. If AI is off for the campaign, the card points you at the toggle instead.

Turn on **Alter ego** (only once **Portrait** is on) to also make a disguised identity: a separate name and a second portrait, seeded from the true-form portrait. This uses twice the picture credits, and the price on the card shows it.

When it finishes, the card shows the new NPC with **Open NPC**. Every field the AI returned is applied: name, species, alignment, age, occupation, status, relationship, tags, appearance, personality, backstory, notes, portrait, and the alter ego's name and portrait if you asked for one.

### Running in the background

You can close the Familiar or move to another page while it works. A badge appears when the NPC is ready. Only one AI generation can run at a time. The button is off while another one is in flight.

## What your players see

Players never see the generator: it's a DM-only creation tool. Once you save or generate an NPC, its visibility to players is exactly what you set afterward (see [NPC Visibility Controls](#npc-visibility-controls)); nothing is shared automatically.

## Tips

> **What to make** is useful even without AI. Quick create saves it straight into the NPC's DM notes, so a reminder you jot there is not wasted.

- A generated NPC's disguise starts unrevealed even when you asked for an alter ego. Reveal it from the sheet when you're ready for players to meet the true form.
