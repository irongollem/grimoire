The Party Tracker (`/party`) is your roster of player characters and companions, with the running HP, initiative, and passive-skill numbers you need between and during sessions.

## Adding characters

- **Add Hero** creates a full party member — a player character with the complete sheet (stats, inventory, spells, and so on).
- **Add Companion** creates a lighter-weight entry for animal companions, hirelings, or familiars that need HP and initiative tracking but not a full character sheet.

## Group Portrait

Below the roster, generate or upload a single shot of the whole party. Reference it with `@Party` inside Chronicler scenes (Scriptorium) instead of dropping in every individual portrait — it saves both tokens and effort when illustrating group scenes.

→ _See Character Codex — Overview_ for the character-build side of a party member, and _Scriptorium — Document Publisher_ for where `@Party` references are used.
