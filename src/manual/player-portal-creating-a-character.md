A character belongs to you, not to a campaign. You can build one before you have joined any table, keep several at once, and bring one to a campaign when you are ready.

## Adventurer's Rest

**Adventurer's Rest** is the player's front door. It is the page you land on when you sign in as a player, and it works even if you have not joined a campaign yet. It has three parts:

- **Your Characters**: every character you own, each on a card showing its class, level and edition, and which campaign it sits in. A character that is not at a table is marked **Resting**.
- **Your Campaigns**: the campaigns you play in, each with a **Play** button.
- **Join a Campaign**: paste an invite link or code and click **Join**.

## Creating a character

1. On Adventurer's Rest, click **New Character**. If you have none yet, click **Create your first character**. (From inside a campaign, **Create Character** on your champions page does the same.)
2. Work through the steps along the top: **Edition**, **Basics**, **Abilities**, **Background**, **Class**, **Equipment**, **Done**. Go on with **Next →**, back with **← Back**, and click a step you have already passed to return to it. **Skip** leaves a step for later.
3. On the last step, click **Begin My Adventure**, or **Begin + Level Up to 2** to start at level 2 straight away.

If you leave before saving, Grimoire asks whether you want to lose what you have entered, so a stray tap does not throw your work away.

### Edition

Choose the edition first (the 2014 or the 2024 rules). The edition decides which species, backgrounds, classes and spells you can pick from. Joining a campaign fixes the books for you: the step says **This table's books decide what you can pick.** Otherwise you can use **Choose books** to pick which books you build from.

### Basics and species

Add a portrait if you like, then fill in **Character Name** (required) and **Player Name**. Below that, the **SPECIES** pickers show a card for each species with its traits, size and walking speed. Click one to choose it. A species that has variants shows **CHOOSE A VARIANT**: pick one, because it sets your size and speed.

### Abilities

Choose how to set your scores with the row at the top: **Point Buy**, **Standard Array**, **Roll 4d6** or **Manual**. Point buy counts **Points remaining**; with **Roll 4d6**, click **Roll Ability Scores** to fill a pool, then assign them. Species bonuses are applied for you, and you can skip or adjust them.

### Background

Click a background card to choose it. Cards show the skills, tools and languages it gives. In the 2024 rules a background also adds to your ability scores (a +2/+1 split or +1/+1/+1) and gives a feat; Grimoire asks you to finish that choice before you move on. If the background's feat comes from a book your table does not use, it says so and asks you to pick another. Further down, fill in alignment, deity, age, appearance, personality traits, ideals, bonds, flaws and backstory.

### Class and equipment

Pick a class, then your saving throws, skills, tool proficiencies and languages. If your class chooses its subclass at level 1, choose it here before you continue. On **Equipment** you can have your chosen loadout and background gear added to your inventory automatically.

If you build a character with no campaign yet, the equipment is added when it joins a table, and the last step tells you so.

## Your pool of characters

Every character you create lands in your pool and stays yours, even if you leave a campaign, are removed from one, or the campaign is deleted. A character is **never deleted** when it leaves a table: it goes back to **Resting**.

Each card has a main button and a **⋯** menu:

- **At a table**: **Continue** opens the character in that campaign. The menu has **Detach** (return it to your pool) and **Make a copy**.
- **Resting**: **Attach** shows your campaigns; pick one to bring the character in. The menu has **Edit**, **Make a copy** and **Delete**.

Delete asks you to confirm and cannot be undone.

## One character, one campaign at a time

A character sits in at most one campaign. Its level, experience, hit points, inventory and gold travel with it. To move it:

1. On its card, open **⋯** and choose **Detach**, then confirm. The character returns to your pool, keeping its progress.
2. On the same card, click **Attach** and pick the other campaign.

Inside a campaign you can also use **Leave campaign** on your champions page, which does the same thing. Things that belong to a specific table, such as that campaign's inventory contents, companions, notes and downtime, stay with the table.

### Same character at two tables

Choose **Make a copy** (**Copy to my pool** on your champions page). The copy keeps your sheet, classes and spells and lands in your pool as a new, resting character. The two then grow separately.

### When a table cannot take your character

Campaigns list their edition beside their name in the **Attach** list. If a table plays a different edition than your character and does not allow mixing, Grimoire stops you and offers a copy converted to the table's edition, leaving your original untouched, or lets you choose another table. Converting can leave a choice for you to confirm, such as a background bonus or a spell, and the sheet shows a notice until you do. A table playing a game system without 5e characters cannot take a 5e character at all.

If the campaign needs the DM's approval for something on your character, its card shows **Waiting for approval**.
