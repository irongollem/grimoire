When the DM starts a fight, your device finds out straight away. There is nothing to refresh.

## When the fight starts

- A message appears: **Roll your initiative** (if you have not rolled yet) or **Encounter Started!**. Tap it to join.
- A pulsing **Live** button appears in the top bar.
- On a **tablet or larger screen**, the encounter panel opens by itself on the left of the screen. Drag its edge to make it wider or narrower.
- On a **phone**, tap **Live** to open the encounter full screen.

The panel stays up to date even if you wander off to another page, and your device chimes when it becomes your turn.

## Rolling initiative

Until you have rolled, the panel leads with **Roll for initiative**, showing d20 plus your Dexterity modifier. Tap **Roll initiative**. The roll goes to chat like any other, and your number slots into the order. Afterwards the panel says **You rolled** your number and that it is waiting for the DM to start. You roll again each fight.

## Before the first turn

While the DM gathers the party, the panel says **Gathering Party…**. If you own companions that can fight, a strip lets you mark each one **Joining** or **Elsewhere** before the fight begins, so the DM does not have to take a benched companion out by hand. Once combat starts, that is locked.

## During the fight

The panel shows the round number, whose turn it is, and the initiative order. On your turn a **YOUR TURN!** banner pulses, the screen gives a small shake, and a chime plays (you can turn the chime off in your settings). Your own hit points and conditions update live as the DM changes them.

Tap any row for a closer look. A person you know opens their card, a character opens their sheet, and an enemy opens an enlarged portrait and name, with a place to jot your own notes on it.

### What you are allowed to see

Everything on the panel is what the DM has shown the party:

- A monster the DM has not revealed does not appear at all.
- A monster the DM has revealed only as a presence appears as **???** until they reveal it.
- How much health you see depends on the campaign's **Health Visibility** setting. On **Strategic** (the default) your party shows exact hit points with a colour bar, and monsters show a word with a bar: **Healthy**, **Hurt**, **Wounded**, **Bloodied** or **Dead**. On **Immersive** the party shows a bar with no numbers and monsters show only the word. On **Unknown** monsters and other non-player creatures show no health at all.
- You can open the stat block of a monster only once the DM has shared it with you.

## The battle map

If the fight is on a place with a map, **View battle map** appears on the panel. It works on a phone as well as a tablet.

- **The header** says **Your turn** with the round and how many feet of movement you have left, or whose turn it is and where you come after them. Your hit points sit on the right. When the map is following someone else, a **Follow** chip lets you turn that off.
- **Following**: on a phone the map zooms in and stays centred on whoever is acting, which is you on your turn. Switch **Follow** off to pan freely.
- **Moving**: tap your own token, then tap the square you want, and confirm with **Move** (or **Cancel**). You can also drag your token. The bar shows the distance against your speed and turns amber or red when you go past it. It is a warning only: the DM has the final say on what is legal, and every square counts as 5 feet because you cannot see the terrain.
- You can move only your own token.
- **The bottom bar** has **Map**, **My sheet** (jump to your character sheet) and **Roll** (the dice roller).

### What the map shows

The map is fogged. You see the parts the party has explored and what the party can see right now, limited by what your characters can actually see in the dark. Your own token always shows. Other tokens, monsters included, appear only where someone in the party can see them. If your table plays with **Each their own** sight, the map shows only what your own character sees.

## For the DM

Players see this view of the fight as long as you have gone live. You can check what they see with **As players see it** on the Play screen. See [Encounter Runner](#encounter-runner) and [Encounter Map: Battle Map & Fog of War](#encounter-map-battle-map-fog-of-war).
