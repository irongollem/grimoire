Can't make it tonight? Ask someone at the table to play your character for the session. They play it on their own account, so nobody shares a password. The character stays yours: it comes back to you when the session ends, and your Hearth tells you what happened while you were away.

## Handing your character over

On your character sheet, open the **⋯** menu and choose **Ask someone to look after {name}**. Pick another player, or the DM, and confirm.

The DM can do the same for you from **Campaign Settings → Members & Invites**, with **Hand over…** on your row.

One person looks after a character at a time. Handing it to someone else ends the earlier hand-over.

## How long it lasts

A hand-over lasts for one session:

- Handed over **during** a session, it lasts until that session ends.
- Handed over **between** sessions, it lasts until the end of the next session.
- If the DM continues a session that was ended by mistake, the hand-over continues with it.

You can take your character back at any time: the sheet says **{player} is looking after {name}**, with **Take back**. The DM can take it back from the Members tab too.

## Looking after someone's character

When a character is handed to you, small round portraits appear in the top bar: your own character and the one you're looking after. The one you're playing is in colour; tap the other to switch. On a keyboard, **Ctrl+Shift+U** (**⌘⇧U** on a Mac) switches to the next one.

While you play it you can do everything that happens at the table: hit points, conditions, death saves, spell slots and casting, preparing spells, rests, class features, Wild Shape, rolls, and claiming loot. Rolls and chat lines read **"Tomas, for Mira"**, so everyone knows who is playing.

What stays with its player: levelling up, editing or rebuilding the character, its backstory and notes, its portrait, weapon masteries and infusions, downtime, retiring it, and moving it to another table. Their private journal and notes stay private too. You see what the character has been shown (handouts, people, places), not what its player wrote for themselves.

When you're done, **Hand back** on the sheet returns it. When the session ends, it goes back on its own, and you're switched back to your own character.

## When you get it back

Your **Hearth** shows who looked after your character and in which session, with a short account of what was rolled for it, for a week afterwards.

## For the DM

- The **Party** tracker shows **with {player}** on a character someone is looking after.
- The **Members** tab shows who has whose character, with **Take back** and **Hand over…**.
- You can play any character at your table yourself, as before, with or without a hand-over.
