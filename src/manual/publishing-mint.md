**The Mint** makes two things: round tokens for your virtual tabletop or for printing as standees, and custom coins to print for your table. Find it in the sidebar under **Publish → The Mint**. It is desktop only. It has two tabs, **Tokens** and **Coins**.

## Tokens tab

Tokens are circular portrait images used on battlemaps, in Roll20, or as physical standees.

### Entity sources

Pick from four sources:

- **Party**: your player characters.
- **NPCs**: any NPC in your tracker.
- **Monsters**: any monster in the bestiary.
- **Custom**: type a name under **Name…**, optionally press **Upload image (optional)** to add a picture, then press **Use → Token Preview**.

Click one in the list to see its token in the preview.

An entity with no picture shows "No art" in the list. When AI is enabled for the campaign, select it and choose **Paint portrait** to generate one from what the entity already says about itself. The portrait is saved onto the NPC, monster or character, so its token, its card and its detail page all gain it at once. It costs credits, shown beside the button, and one portrait is painted at a time. Library monsters are read-only and cannot be painted here; customize a copy first.

If an NPC or monster has a cutout (the figure on a transparent background), a **Picture** / **Cutout** switch appears under the preview so you can choose the portrait or the whole figure on the token. An entity with only a cutout shows it in the list instead of "No art".

### Token settings

- **Ring Colour**: six presets (Party blue, Ally gold, Enemy red, Neutral grey, Boss purple, Nature green) or a custom colour picker. The ring colour-codes the token's faction.
- **Ring Width**: Thin, Medium, Thick, or Heavy.
- **Name Label**: switch **On** or **Off** a curved name arc along the bottom of the token over a dark gradient band.
- **Export Size**: **280px · Roll20 1×1** or **512px · HD / Large** (for large creatures, big displays or printing).

### Export

- **Download PNG**: saves the token as a file. Upload it to your VTT afterwards (for example Roll20's My Library, Foundry's file picker, or Owlbear Rodeo's image drop).
- **Copy**: copies the image for pasting directly into Roll20 or other VTTs.

### Print queue

Press **+ Add to Print Sheet** under the preview to put the token in the queue, then add as many more as you like. The **Print Sheet (n tokens)** block appears below. Remove a token from it with the **✕** beside its name.

- **Back style**: **Mystery ?** (a dark disc in the ring colour with a "?", which hides identity until flipped) or **Mirror** (same image as the front).
- **Print size**: **25mm**, **32mm** or **50mm**.
- Press **Print Sheet** to print.
- Backs are duplex-aligned so long-edge flipping produces correctly reversed columns.

Cut circles with a craft cutter or scissors. Laminate for durability.

## Coins tab

Design custom campaign coins, useful as physical currency tokens, quest reward props, or faction emblems.

### Coin settings

- **Metal**: Copper, Silver, Electrum, Gold or Platinum. The denomination label updates to match (CP, SP, EP, GP, PP) unless you have typed your own.
- **Centre Value**: free text shown in the centre of the coin face, for example 10.
- **Emblem**: pick a symbol: Crown, Cross, Fleur, Star, Anchor, Moon, Diamond, Omega or Knight, or **None**. Hover one to see its name.
- **Denomination Label**: free text for the coin type shown on the face, for example GP.
- **Rim Text**: text curved around the outer edge of the coin (e.g. campaign name, kingdom motto).

### Coin preview

A live coin preview updates as you type, showing the metal, rim text, emblem, centre value and denomination label, with the size and how many fit on a sheet beneath it.

### Print Size

- **Small** (24mm): about 70 per sheet.
- **Standard** (30mm): about 48 per sheet.
- **Large** (38mm): about 35 per sheet.

Press **Print Sheet** under the preview. The print output includes a front sheet and a duplex-aligned back sheet. Print on card stock for best results.
