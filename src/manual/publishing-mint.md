**The Mint** (`/mint`) creates two types of physical game assets: circular combat tokens and custom campaign coins.

## Tokens tab

Tokens are circular portrait images used on battlemaps, in Roll20, or as physical standees.

### Entity sources

Pick from four sources:

- **Party** — your player characters.
- **NPCs** — any NPC in your tracker.
- **Monsters** — any monster in the bestiary.
- **Custom** — enter a name and optionally upload a local image.

### Token settings

- **Ring colour** — six presets (Party blue, Ally gold, Enemy red, Neutral grey, Boss purple, Nature green) or a custom colour picker. The ring colour-codes the token's faction.
- **Ring width** — Thin, Medium, Thick, or Heavy.
- **Name label** — toggle a curved name arc along the bottom of the token over a dark gradient band.
- **Export size** — 280px (Roll20 1×1 map tile) or 512px (HD for large displays or printing).

### Export

- **Download PNG** — saves the token as a file.
- **Copy to Clipboard** — copies the image for pasting directly into Roll20 or other VTTs.

### Print queue

Add multiple tokens to the print queue, then print a sheet:

- **Back style** — Mystery (dark disc with a "?" glyph, hides identity until flipped) or Mirror (same image as front).
- **Print size** — 25mm (~70/sheet), 32mm (~48/sheet), or 50mm (~20/sheet).
- Backs are duplex-aligned so long-edge flipping produces correctly reversed columns.

Cut circles with a craft cutter or scissors. Laminate for durability.

## Coins tab

Design custom campaign coins — useful as physical currency tokens, quest reward props, or faction emblems.

### Coin settings

- **Metal** — Gold, Silver, Copper, Platinum, Electrum, or Iron (denomination label auto-updates).
- **Centre value** — freeform text displayed in the centre of the coin face.
- **Emblem/motif** — pick a preset symbol: crown, skull, dragon, star, sword, shield, flame, leaf, anchor, moon, sun, eye, or None.
- **Denomination label** — freeform text for the coin type shown on the face.
- **Rim text** — text curved around the outer edge of the coin (e.g. campaign name, kingdom motto).

### Coin preview

A live SVG coin (200×200px) updates as you type. The coin renders as a realistic metallic disc with rim text, emblem, centre value, and denomination label.

### Print sizes

- **Small 24mm** — ~70 per A4 sheet.
- **Standard 30mm** — ~48 per A4 sheet.
- **Large 38mm** — ~35 per A4 sheet.

The print output includes a front sheet and a duplex-aligned back sheet. Print on card stock for best results.
