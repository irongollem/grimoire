The **Custom Rules** section of the Reliquary (`/rules` → Custom Rules tab) lets you write, organise, and share your campaign's house rules. Players see the rules you mark as visible in their own Reliquary view.

## Creating a house rule

Click **New Rule**. Fill in:

- **Title** — a short, clear name (e.g. "Gritty Realism Rests", "Death at Negative Max HP").
- **Category** — group related rules together (e.g. Combat, Exploration, Social, Magic, Resting).
- **Tags** — freeform labels for searching.
- **Player Visible** toggle — when on, players see this rule in their `/play/rules` view.
- **Body** — rich text. Write the full rule text here. Use headings, lists, and bold text to make it scannable.

Optionally add a **Tracker** to the rule (see _Custom Rules: Trackers_).

## Organising rules

Rules are searchable by title, category, and tags. Use consistent category names across your rules — they'll be grouped in the player view.

The player view shows only rules with `is_player_visible = true`, making it safe to write DM-only notes as non-visible rules (e.g. "Secret faction reputation mechanics") while sharing the player-facing rules clearly.

## Editing and deleting

Click a rule card to open it. Use the **Edit** button to modify any field. **Delete** removes the rule entirely — there's no archive or recycle bin.

## The DM Screen

The Reliquary also has a **DM Screen** tab — a built-in quick-reference grid of standard D&D rules: conditions, combat actions, cover, light, skills, DCs, exhaustion, death saves, travel pace, spell slots. This is always available regardless of custom rules.

## The Compendium

The **Compendium** tab contains the full SRD 2024 ruleset (~317 entries) browsable by category or searchable by keyword. Use this as your primary rules reference during play.
