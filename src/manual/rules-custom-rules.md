The **Custom Rules** tab of the Reliquary (**Compendium → Reliquary**, `/rules` → Custom Rules) is where you write your campaign's own rulings, house rules and homebrew systems, such as weather tables, crafting rules or a sanity track. You decide which rules your players can read: they see only the ones you mark visible, in their own Reliquary.

## Active optional rules

If your campaign has any of Grimoire's built-in optional rules switched on (Campaign Settings → Rules, for example XP levelling or Crafting), they show up at the top of this tab under **Active Optional Rules**, each with a short summary you can expand for the full description. These aren't rules you write yourself; this section just tells you what's currently on.

## Creating a house rule

## How do I write a house rule?

1. Open the **Custom Rules** tab and click **New Rule** (or **Write your first rule** if the tab is empty).
2. Fill in:

   - **Title**: a short, clear name (e.g. "Gritty Realism Rests", "Death at Negative Max HP").
   - **Category**: Combat, Exploration, Social, Crafting, Magic, Environment, Economy or Other.
   - **Tags**: freeform labels for searching.
   - **Visible to Players**: tick it and players can read this rule in their portal. It starts unticked.
   - **Content**: rich text. Write the full rule text here. Use headings, lists, and bold text to make it scannable.
3. Optionally attach a **Tracker** to the rule: see [Custom Rule Trackers](#custom-rule-trackers).
4. Click **Save Rule**. **Cancel** leaves without saving.

## Drafting a house rule with AI

When AI is enabled for your campaign, the Custom Rules tab also has a **Generate** button beside **New Rule**. It opens the **House Rule Generator**. Describe the idea in a sentence or two, such as "characters who see horrors lose Lucidity and act worse as it drops", and optionally pick a **Category**. Grimoire drafts the rule for you and opens it in the editor.

The draft is written as a house rule for your campaign's ruleset (2014 or 2024) and your campaign setting. It has a short summary, then three headings: **When it applies**, **Effect** (with the numbers) and **Exceptions** (what it does not change). Generating costs credits, shown on the button, and the rule is created hidden from players, so you can read and change it before sharing.

Leave **Allow a tracker** on and Grimoire adds a [Tracker](#custom-rule-trackers) when the rule follows a value over time, such as sanity, corruption or hunger, with levels and DM buttons filled in. Turn it off if you only want the rule text. A draft is only a starting point: check the numbers against how your table plays, and edit anything you like. Once you change an AI-drafted rule, it is marked as edited.

## Organising rules

Use **Search custom rules…** and the category filter (**All categories**) to find a rule, and the layout toggle to switch between cards and rows. **Clear** resets the filters. Rules that players can read show a small eye mark on the card. Use consistent category names across your rules: they'll be grouped the same way in the player view.

The player view shows only rules marked **Visible to Players**, making it safe to write DM-only notes as non-visible rules (e.g. "Secret faction reputation mechanics") while sharing the player-facing rules clearly.

## Editing and deleting

Click a rule card to read it, then **Edit** to change any field and **Save Rule** to keep the changes. **Delete** removes the rule entirely, after you confirm: there's no archive or recycle bin.

## What your players see

Players get the same house-rule list in their own Reliquary (**House Rules** tab), filtered to only the rules you've marked **Visible to Players**, with any attached tracker's widget on their own character sheet. They can never create, edit, or see hidden rules.

## Tips

> The Reliquary also has a **DM Screen** tab (static SRD quick-reference) and a **Compendium** tab (the full SRD text): Custom Rules is specifically for the rulings and systems that are yours. See [Reliquary: Overview](#reliquary-overview) for how all five tabs fit together.
