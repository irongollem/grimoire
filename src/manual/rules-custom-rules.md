The **Custom Rules** tab of the Reliquary (**Compendium → Reliquary**, `/rules` → Custom Rules) lets you write, organise, and share your campaign's house rules. Players see the rules you mark as visible in their own Reliquary view.

## Active optional rules

If your campaign has any of Grimoire's built-in optional rules switched on (Campaign Settings → Rules, for example XP levelling or Crafting), they show up at the top of this tab under **Active Optional Rules**, each with a short summary you can expand for the full description. These aren't rules you write yourself; this section just tells you what's currently on.

## Creating a house rule

Click **New Rule**. Fill in:

- **Title**: a short, clear name (e.g. "Gritty Realism Rests", "Death at Negative Max HP").
- **Category**: group related rules together (e.g. Combat, Exploration, Social, Magic, Resting).
- **Tags**: freeform labels for searching.
- **Visible to Players** toggle: when on, players see this rule in their own Reliquary.
- **Content**: rich text. Write the full rule text here. Use headings, lists, and bold text to make it scannable.

Optionally attach a **Tracker** to the rule: see [Custom Rule Trackers](#custom-rule-trackers).

## Organising rules

Rules are searchable by title, category, and tags. Use consistent category names across your rules: they'll be grouped the same way in the player view.

The player view shows only rules marked **Visible to Players**, making it safe to write DM-only notes as non-visible rules (e.g. "Secret faction reputation mechanics") while sharing the player-facing rules clearly.

## Editing and deleting

Click a rule card to open it, then **Edit** to modify any field. **Delete** removes the rule entirely: there's no archive or recycle bin.

## What your players see

Players get the same house-rule list in their own Reliquary (**House Rules** tab), filtered to only the rules you've marked **Visible to Players**, with any attached tracker's widget on their own character sheet. They can never create, edit, or see hidden rules.

## Tips

> The Reliquary also has a **DM Screen** tab (static SRD quick-reference) and a **Compendium** tab (the full SRD text): Custom Rules is specifically for the rulings and systems that are yours. See [Reliquary: Overview](#reliquary-overview) for how all five tabs fit together.
