The **DM Screen** tab in the Reliquary (`/rules` → DM Screen) is a built-in, always-available quick-reference — the tables you'd otherwise keep on a physical screen at the table.

## What's covered

Switch between sections with the tab row at the top:

- **Conditions** — every 5e condition and its mechanical effect.
- **Combat actions** — the standard action list (Attack, Dash, Disengage, Dodge, Help, Hide, Ready, Search, etc.).
- **Cover, light & vision** — cover bonuses, lightly/heavily obscured rules.
- **Skills & DCs** — the skill list and typical difficulty-class bands.
- **Exhaustion & death saves** — level effects and the death-save table.
- **Travel pace & spell slots** — movement rates by pace, and the spell-slots-per-level table.

This reference is static — it isn't affected by your campaign's custom rules and needs no setup. For house rules that override or extend the SRD, use the **Custom Rules** tab instead.
