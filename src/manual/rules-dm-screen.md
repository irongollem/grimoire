The **DM Screen** tab in the Reliquary (**Compendium → Reliquary**, `/rules` → DM Screen) is a built-in, always-available quick-reference: the tables you'd otherwise keep on a physical screen at the table.

## What's covered

Switch between five sections with the button row at the top, then between that section's reference tables underneath:

- **Combat**: actions in combat, common bonus actions, common reactions, cover, conditions, death saving throws, creature size & space, object AC, object HP, underwater combat.
- **Ability Checks**: skills by ability, difficulty class bands, common contests, carrying capacity, jumping.
- **Environment**: light & vision, exhaustion, falling, travel pace, food & water, resting.
- **Spellcasting**: spell slot levels by class level, concentration.
- **Equipment & Services**: lifestyle expenses per day, food/drink/lodging, services, mounts, waterborne vehicles, donning & doffing armor.

This reference is static: it isn't affected by your campaign's custom rules and needs no setup. For house rules that override or extend the SRD, use the **Custom Rules** tab instead.

## How do I...

### Look up a rule in the middle of a fight

1. Open **Compendium → Reliquary** in the sidebar. **DM Screen** is the first tab.
2. Click the section you need: **Combat**, **Ability Checks**, **Environment**, **Spellcasting** or **Equipment & Services**.
3. Scan the tables. On a wide screen they sit in two columns so you can read a name and its description side by side.

For example, for what a condition does, go to **Combat** and find **Conditions**. For a difficulty number, go to **Ability Checks** and find **Difficulty Class**. For how long a party can march in a day, go to **Environment** and find **Travel Pace**.

You can move between sections with the left and right arrow keys once one of the section buttons has focus.

### Find the full text of a rule

The screen is a summary. For the complete wording, use the **Compendium** tab, which has the full rules text, and search it there.

### Keep my own house rule on the screen

The screen is fixed and does not change with your campaign. To keep a house rule, write it on the **Custom Rules** tab. You can mark it visible to players, so it also appears in their rules reference.

### Find out how a Grimoire feature works

Open the **DM Manual** tab, the manual you are reading. Pick a page from the list on the left, or type in the **Search manual…** box.

## Searching the manual with a question

The search box matches words in page titles, summaries and text, so a short phrase such as "hide NPC" or "fog" finds pages directly.

If you type a whole question, such as "how do I hide an NPC from the players", and no page contains those exact words, Grimoire can suggest pages instead. Up to three appear under **Suggested pages**, best match first. A few things to know:

- It tries this only once you have typed at least 8 characters and paused for a moment.
- It needs AI switched on for the campaign. With AI off, nothing you type is sent anywhere, and you see **No matches.**
- It costs you no credits.
- Ordinary word search always wins: Suggested pages appears only when plain search finds nothing.

On a phone, the page list fills the screen until you open a page, and **All pages** takes you back to the list.
