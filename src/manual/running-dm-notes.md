Every entity you can open has a **DM notes** box: one private note that only you can see. It sits on the entity's own page, saves itself as you type, and is there whether you are reading the page or editing it. You never have to open an editor to jot something down.

## Key ideas

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| DM notes box | The note on an entity's page. It reads **DM notes** with **Only you see this** beside it. |
| Scratchpad | A side panel that shows the DM note of whatever you are looking at, so you can write while you browse. |
| Pin | Keeps one note in the scratchpad while you move to other pages. |
| This session | The list of everything you wrote a note on since the running session started. |

DM notes are not the same as **Campaign Notes**. Session notes are written for the table and can be shared with players; a DM note is for you and is never shown to anyone else, in any form. There is no sharing switch.

## Where you find a DM note

The box appears on the page of an NPC, a monster you made, an item you made, a trap, a dungeon feature, a puzzle, a loot table, a roll table, a place in the Atlas, a deity, a species you made, a faction, a companion, a quest (on the **Overview** tab), an encounter (its page and the run screen), and a party member. It shows on phones too.

Your own monsters, items and species have one; the shared library entries do not, because they are not yours to write on.

> A party member's DM note is separate from the character's own **Notes**. Players can read the character's Notes. They can never read yours.

## Writing a note

Click in the box and type. Underneath, a status line reads **Saving…** and then **Saved**. There is no Save button and nothing to cancel: close the page and the note is kept.

The notes you used to fill in on an editor's form (**DM Notes** on a trap, an item or an NPC, **DM Secrets** on a deity) now live in this box, and whatever you had written there is already in it.

## The scratchpad

Open it from the **DM notes** button in the top bar or sidebar, from the **More** sheet on a phone, or with the keyboard shortcut **⌘;** (**Ctrl+;** on Windows and Linux). Press **Esc** to close it.

On a wide screen it docks to the right edge of the window. On a phone it slides up as a sheet from the bottom.

### It follows you

Open an NPC and the scratchpad shows that NPC's note. Move to a place and it switches to the place. The header shows what kind of entity it is and its name. If nothing is open it says to open an NPC, place or item and its notes will appear.

While the scratchpad shows an entity, the box on that entity's page folds into a small line reading **Open in the scratchpad**, so there is only one place to type. Close the scratchpad and the box comes back.

### Pinning a note

Click the pin in the scratchpad's header (tooltip **Pin: keep this note while you browse**) to hold the current note. You can then click through the Atlas, the Bestiary and your quests with the same note beside you, which suits a note you are reading from while you run a scene.

While the panel is pinned and you are looking at something else, a **Switch to** button appears with that entity's name. Click it to go back to following the page, or click the pin again (now **Pinned: unpin**).

### This session

Below the note, the scratchpad lists every entity whose DM note you wrote on since the current session started, newest first, with how long ago. Each row shows the entity's name and what kind of thing it is, and clicking it takes you there. The note you are looking at is marked **Showing now**.

When the session ends the list is headed **Last session** and keeps showing that session's entries until you start the next one. With no session started, there is no list. See [Running a Session](#running-a-session).

This is meant for the end of the evening: everything you jotted down during play, in one place, ready to fold into next week's prep.

## Tips

- AI generators that create an NPC, a trap, a loot table and the like still put their GM-side asides in the DM note. The deity generator writes its generated secrets there as well.
- NPC **Quick Create** saves its **Concept** box as the new NPC's DM note.
- The note works the same way on every entity, so a habit learned on NPCs carries over to places and quests.
