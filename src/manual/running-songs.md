Sometimes the music is for the table, not for you. **Songs** gives every player a page of big picture buttons that play your campaign's music tracks on their own phone, whenever they like. It needs no session and no DM at the board. It began with a young player who could not read yet and wanted to play the tavern song himself.

## Sharing a playlist

Only music playlists can be shared.

1. Open the **Soundboard** page and edit a music playlist (**Edit playlist**).
2. Tick **Players can listen**. The hint says "Everyone at the table can play these songs on their own phone, on the Songs page."
3. Press **Save playlist**. **Open the Songs page** appears under the box to take you straight there and see what your players see.

Once a playlist is shared, a **Songs** button sits at the top of the Soundboard page, so you can open the Songs page any time without opening a playlist.

Untick it and the playlist disappears from their Songs page.

Spotify tracks cannot be shared this way. Only sounds you have in Grimoire are listed, and only those that belong to this campaign.

## What players see

The player's **Settings** shows a **Songs** card with **Open Songs** once something has been shared. Until then the card is hidden. The Songs page itself is chrome-free:

- Each song is a big picture button, two to a row. Its own cover art is used when it has one. Otherwise it gets a picture on a colour that stays the same for that song. The name sits small underneath, for a grown-up.
- Tapping a song plays it. Tap again to pause, again to carry on. A song plays to its end and stops. There is no queue, shuffle or repeat, so what plays is always the button just pressed.
- Each shared playlist is its own page. Swipe sideways to change playlist and scroll up and down for the songs. With more than one playlist, a bar along the bottom shows each one as a small circle to tap.
- The lock screen shows the song while it plays.
- A song that cannot play shows a muted speaker. If the page says "The songs did not load.", **Try again** reloads it.

Players must be signed in. There is no public link, so nothing about your campaign can be reached without an account. The page shows the songs of the campaign you have open in Grimoire. Pinned to a home screen it has no open campaign, so someone in several campaigns sees the songs of each there, with the campaign's name above them.

### Pinning it on a phone

Open Songs on the phone and use the browser's **Add to Home Screen**. It pins as **Grimoire Songs**, its own little app that opens straight to the songs rather than the front door. The first time, a parent signs in once inside the pinned app, since a home screen app keeps its own sign-in.

Older phones that cannot run Grimoire itself are sent to a simpler version of the same page.

## Songs and shared playback

These are different. **Songs** is any time, by the player's own choice. **Join audio** (switched on in Board settings) is one live stream of the track you are playing, so the whole table hears it together. Use Songs for the little ones' favourites, and Join audio for the party in different rooms.

## Grimoire Sounds

You do not start with an empty grid. Grimoire hosts a library of free sounds, **Grimoire Sounds**, under every plan. They do not count against your sound quota.

- In the Soundboard's library, press **Add sound** and choose the **Grimoire Sounds** tab. Search for a sound or browse a theme such as rain, tavern or dungeon, then add it to your board.
- **Start with a stocked board** (on an empty board) adds a set of ready-made scenes. If you have some already, it reads **Add the rest of the starter scenes**. Press **Add n scenes**. Adding them twice does nothing, and a layer two scenes share is only made once.

The credit for each sound travels with it, because some of these sounds require crediting their makers. It shows where you add and list sounds. The full list is on the Grimoire Sounds credits page and under the Reliquary's Licences tab. You do not need to credit anyone yourself.

A sound from the library you share through Songs works the same as one you uploaded.
