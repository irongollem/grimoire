Grimoire has two halves. Most of the time you are **preparing** — writing NPCs, drawing the map, laying out a quest. That is just the app; it needs no mode and no switch.

When your players sit down, you **start a session**. The control is in the sidebar under the Grimoire title (on a phone or tablet it is in the top bar, and in the **All sections** sheet).

## What changes while a session runs

Four things, and the app lists them for you the first time you start one.

- **Revealing an NPC announces it.** Showing a character to the party posts a line into your players' chat — _"The party encounters Sephek Kaltro."_ While no session is running, the same reveal is silent.
- **The soundboard switches to Perform.** Big pads with artwork instead of edit controls and drag handles. To rearrange the board mid-evening, end the session or use the Soundboard page's own controls.
- **Quests open on the run cockpit** — where the party is standing — instead of the quest's overview.
- **The bottom bar reshuffles** on touch devices: table-side sections come first, and the centre button becomes the dice roller instead of ＋.

## Starting one without meaning to

Hitting **Go Live** on an encounter starts a session if one isn't already running. You are at the table if you are running combat, and being made to say so twice is the sort of bookkeeping nobody thanks an app for. Grimoire tells you it did it.

## Ending it matters

While a session runs, your reveals reach your players. A session you forgot to end is still broadcasting on Wednesday afternoon, so the reveal you make while prepping next week's dungeon lands in the party's chat.

The rail shows how long the session has been running, which is usually enough to notice. If one has been running for more than six hours, Grimoire asks whether you are still playing next time you open the app.

**Ending a session closes what it contains:**

- Any running encounter stops.
- Every open quest chain **pauses at its current beat** — nothing is lost. Next session you pick each one up exactly where the party left it, and the paused chains are listed on your dashboard in the meantime.

## Sessions and the Player portal

"Session" here means the live evening. It is not the same as:

- **Session dates** in Campaign settings, where the table agrees which evening to play.
- **Player mode**, the switch between your DM view and the player portal at `/play`.

Your players never see a session control. What they see is the consequence: reveals arriving in chat while you are running the game.
