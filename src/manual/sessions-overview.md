Grimoire has two halves. Most of the time you are **preparing** — writing NPCs, drawing the map, laying out a quest. That is just the app; it needs no mode and no switch.

When your players sit down, you **start a session**. The control is the **Start session** button in the sidebar under the Grimoire title (on a phone or tablet it is in the top bar, and also in the **All sections** sheet).

## Key ideas

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Session | The live evening — started and ended explicitly by you. This is the only thing this page calls a "session." |
| Session dates | A different feature entirely — the real-world date your table has agreed to play next, set in Campaign Settings and shown on the dashboard's Next Session card. |
| Player mode | The DM ↔ Player portal switch (`/play`). Unrelated to whether a session is running. |
| Session day/location | The in-world game day and current location shown on the dashboard's Session widget — this advances whether or not a session is running. |

## Starting a session

1. Click **Start session** in the sidebar (or top bar / More sheet on touch devices).
2. The first time you do this, a dialog titled **Start the session** lists the four things that change (see below) and offers a **Don't show this again** checkbox. Click **Start session** in the dialog to confirm, or **Cancel** to back out.
3. Once running, the control becomes a status pill reading **Session live** with an elapsed-time clock next to it.

### Starting one without meaning to

Clicking **Go Live** on an encounter starts a session automatically if one isn't already running. You are at the table if you are running combat, and being made to say so twice is bookkeeping nobody thanks an app for.

## What changes while a session runs

The four things the start dialog lists for you:

- **Reveals announce themselves.** Showing an NPC to the party posts a line into your players' chat — _"The party encounters Sephek Kaltro."_ While no session is running, the same reveal is silent.
- **The soundboard switches to Perform.** Big pads with artwork instead of edit controls and drag handles. To rearrange the board mid-evening, end the session or use the Soundboard page's own controls.
- **Quests open on the run cockpit** — where the party is standing — instead of the quest's premise/overview.
- **The bottom bar reshuffles** on touch devices: table-side sections come first, and the centre button becomes the dice roller instead of ＋.

## Ending a session

1. Click the **✕** at the right of the **Session live** pill (its tooltip reads **End session**).

Ending closes what the session contains:

- Any running encounter stops.
- Every open quest chain **pauses at its current beat** — nothing is lost. Next session you pick each one up exactly where the party left it, and the paused chains are listed on your dashboard in the meantime.

A toast confirms what happened — e.g. "Session ended — 1 encounter stopped, 2 quests paused."

### Ending it matters

While a session runs, your reveals reach your players. A session you forgot to end is still broadcasting on Wednesday afternoon, so the reveal you make while prepping next week's dungeon lands in the party's chat.

The live rail (under the session control) shows how long the session has been running, which is usually enough to notice. If one has been running for more than six hours, Grimoire asks **Still playing?** the next time you open the app — a dialog, not a toast, because the question has real consequences and a toast that times out would default to "keep broadcasting." Answer **Still playing** to keep it running or **End session** to close it.

## The live rail

Below the session control, a small indented list shows everything currently running inside the session, ranked by urgency:

- **Combat · round N** — a live encounter, linking to its run screen.
- **N quest(s) in play** — chains the party is actively walking (a paused chain doesn't count; that's a prep concern and belongs on the dashboard instead).
- **N sound(s) playing** — audio from the soundboard or Spotify.
- **Writing a draft** — an AI generation in progress.

## What your players see

"Session" here means the live evening, not a mode your players ever touch. They never see a session control themselves. What they see is the consequence:

- **"⚔️ The session begins."** posted to chat when you start (not repeated if a session is already running).
- A quiet **"Session live"** indicator in their portal header — it yields to a live-encounter pill if combat is also running, since combat is the more urgent thing to flag.
- Reveals arriving in their chat while you are running the game.

## Tips

> If you're prepping on a Sunday afternoon and revealing an NPC just to check how it looks, make sure no session is still running from Wednesday — otherwise that reveal reaches your players' chat right now.

- The dice roller in the sidebar's brand row is always available; it isn't tied to a session, because it's a tool you reach for, not a thing that runs.
- Session dates (the real-world "when do we play next" agreement) live in [Campaign Settings](#campaign-settings), not here.
