Grimoire has two halves. Most of the time you are **preparing**: writing NPCs, drawing the map, laying out a quest. That is just the app; it needs no mode and no switch.

When your players sit down, you **start a session**. The control is the **Start session** button in the sidebar under the Grimoire title (on a phone or tablet it is in the top bar, and also in the **All sections** sheet).

## Key ideas

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Session | One evening at the table: started and ended explicitly by you, and kept in the Sessions log afterwards. This is the only thing this page calls a "session." |
| Session number | A label you give a session ("Session 15"). It is only a name: nothing counts for you, and two sessions may share one. |
| Session dates | A different feature entirely: the real-world date your table has agreed to play next, set in Campaign Settings and shown on the dashboard's Next Session card. |
| Player mode | The DM ↔ Player portal switch (`/play`). Unrelated to whether a session is running. |
| Session day/location | The in-world game day and current location shown on the dashboard's Session widget: this advances whether or not a session is running. |

## Starting a session

1. Click **Start session** in the sidebar (or top bar / More sheet on touch devices), or the **Start session** button on the dashboard's Sessions widget.
2. A dialog titled **Start the session** asks which session it is:
   - **Session number** is filled in with one more than your highest so far. Change it if you like.
   - **Title** is optional. If today is a confirmed session date on your calendar, its title is filled in, and that scheduled session is marked as the one that happened.
   - A **Last time** line reminds you which session you played last.
3. Click **Start session** (the button names the number, for example **Start session 15**) to begin, or **Cancel** to back out.
4. Once running, the control becomes a status pill reading **Session 15 live** (or **Session live** for an unnumbered one) with an elapsed-time clock next to it.

### Numbering

The number is yours. Grimoire suggests the next one, but never insists: you can skip a number, repeat one (a one-shot, a session you replayed) or leave it out. To start a session with no number at all, such as a test run while you are setting up, click **Start without a number**. You can number it later from the Sessions log, or delete it.

### Starting one without meaning to

Clicking **Go Live** on an encounter starts a session automatically if one isn't already running, without asking for a number. You are at the table if you are running combat, and being made to say so twice is bookkeeping nobody thanks an app for. The session shows up in the log as unnumbered, so you can number it afterwards.

## What changes while a session runs

The four things the start dialog lists for you:

- **Reveals announce themselves.** Showing an NPC to the party posts a line into your players' chat: *"The party encounters Sephek Kaltro."* While no session is running, the same reveal is silent.
- **The soundboard switches to Perform.** Big pads with artwork instead of edit controls and drag handles. To rearrange the board mid-evening, end the session or use the Soundboard page's own controls.
- **Quests open on the run cockpit** (where the party is standing) instead of the quest's premise/overview.
- **The bottom bar reshuffles** on touch devices: table-side sections come first, and the centre button becomes the dice roller instead of ＋.

## Ending a session

1. Click the **✕** at the right of the **Session live** pill (its tooltip reads **End session**).

Ending closes what the session contains:

- Any running encounter stops.
- Every open quest chain **pauses at its current beat**: nothing is lost. Next session you pick each one up exactly where the party left it, and the paused chains are listed on your dashboard in the meantime.

A toast confirms what happened, e.g. "Session ended: 1 encounter stopped, 2 quests paused." The session stays in your log.

### Ending it matters

While a session runs, your reveals reach your players. A session you forgot to end is still broadcasting on Wednesday afternoon, so the reveal you make while prepping next week's dungeon lands in the party's chat.

The live rail (under the session control) shows how long the session has been running, which is usually enough to notice. If one has been running for more than six hours, Grimoire asks **Still playing?** the next time you open the app: a dialog, not a toast, because the question has real consequences and a toast that times out would default to "keep broadcasting." Answer **Still playing** to keep it running or **End session** to close it.

## The Sessions section

**Sessions** sits at the bottom of the Campaign group in the sidebar, above Settings. It remembers every evening.

- **The log** lists your sessions, newest first, each with its date, how long it ran and what came of it (people met, combat). A session with no notes yet shows **Write notes**. A session you started without a number shows **Number it** and **Delete**, so test runs do not clutter the log. Two sessions with the same number sit side by side.
- **Add a past session** logs one you played before the log existed or away from the app: a number, a title and the date.
- **A session's page** lets you change its number and title, shows its note (or **Write the notes**, **Draft with the Chronicler** and **Link an existing note**), and lists what the party learned. **Delete this session** removes it from the log; its note stays, just no longer linked.

### What the party learned

A session's page lists what came out in it: **People met**, **Places**, **Handouts**, **Creatures discovered**, **Quests** and **Combat**. You do not log any of this by hand. When you share an NPC, a place or a handout, or the party discovers a creature or moves a quest forward, Grimoire files it under the session that is running. Things you share while preparing, between sessions, are filed under the next session you start.

Two ways to correct it:

- **Another session...** on any entry moves it to a different session. Use it if you shared something at the table after the session had ended, or in the wrong week.
- **+ Something they learned that you forgot to share** is for when you told the table something out loud and never shared it in the app. Pick what it was and who learned it, and Grimoire shares it now and files it under this session.

### Learned outside any session

Anything shared before Grimoire kept the log has no session. **Learned outside any session** (open it from the log's summary line) lists each of these with a suggested session, and a bulk **Put them in** to move several at once. Whatever you leave there shows to your players under **Before the log**. Moments from before the log are dated by when the NPC or place was created, which may not be the evening your players actually met it, so treat the suggestions as a starting point.

### The Sessions widget on the dashboard

The **Sessions** widget replaces the old Last session widget. It shows your next session (or the one running now, with **End**), what happened **Last time**, which sessions still have no notes, and how many things were learned outside any session. A quick line under it adds a sentence to the session's notes: jot it down at the table and find it in the note afterwards. If your dashboard had Last session on it, Sessions took its place.

## The live rail

Below the session control, a small indented list shows everything currently running inside the session, ranked by urgency:

- **Combat · round N**: a live encounter, linking to its run screen.
- **N quest(s) in play**: chains the party is actively walking (a paused chain doesn't count; that's a prep concern and belongs on the dashboard instead).
- **N sound(s) playing**: audio from the soundboard or Spotify.
- **Writing a draft**: an AI generation in progress.

## What your players see

"Session" here means the live evening, not a mode your players ever touch. They never see a session control themselves. What they see is the consequence:

- **"⚔️ The session begins."** posted to chat when you start (not repeated if a session is already running).
- A quiet **"Session live"** indicator in their portal header (it says "Session 15" when the session has a number): it yields to a live-encounter pill if combat is also running, since combat is the more urgent thing to flag.
- Reveals arriving in their chat while you are running the game.
- On their People page, **Met** groups the people they know by session ("Session 14", with its title and date). People filed under no session appear under **Before the log**.

## Tips

> If you're prepping on a Sunday afternoon and revealing an NPC just to check how it looks, make sure no session is still running from Wednesday: otherwise that reveal reaches your players' chat right now.

- The dice roller in the sidebar's brand row is always available; it isn't tied to a session, because it's a tool you reach for, not a thing that runs.
- Session dates (the real-world "when do we play next" agreement) live in [Campaign Settings](#campaign-settings), not here.
