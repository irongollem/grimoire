If your table plays on a rhythm ("every other Thursday", "the first Saturday of the month"), you do not have to propose each date and wait for answers. Set the rhythm once. Grimoire then only asks the table when an evening *changes*.

Everything for the DM is in **Campaign Settings**, on the **Scheduling** tab.

## Setting a rhythm

At the top of the Scheduling tab, press **Set a regular rhythm**.

1. Choose **Weekly** or **Monthly**.
2. Weekly: pick **How often**: **Every week**, **Every other week**, **Every 3 weeks** or **Every 4 weeks**. Monthly: pick **Which week**: **First**, **Second**, **Third**, **Fourth** or **Last**.
3. Pick the **Day** of the week.
4. Set the **Start time** and **Duration (hours)**.
5. Give it a **Title**. The default is "Session".
6. Set **Starts on**, and optionally **Ends on (optional)**. The editor shows how many evenings fall inside the dates.
7. Press **Save rhythm**.

The summary then shows your rhythm and the next few dates. The pencil edits it and the bin removes it.

If you change the rhythm later, the new one starts from today, so evenings you have already played are left alone.

Regular evenings are not emailed and send no reminder. They simply appear as planned. Email is kept for planning changes, and the repeating event in each player's own calendar does the reminding.

## Moving or cancelling one evening

Regular evenings are listed under **Upcoming Sessions**, each marked **Regular**. Two buttons deal with a single evening:

- **Move** opens a date and time picker headed "Move ... to:". Press **Move evening**. Grimoire sends your players the ordinary proposal email with its yes and no answer, and an announcement in the app. The moved evening reads "Moved from ..." and is a suggestion until you press **Confirm** on it, like any proposed date.
- **Cancel evening** removes that one evening. Players get an announcement and the evening leaves their calendar. Nothing is asked of them.

Cancelled evenings are listed under **Cancelled**. Press **Restore** to bring the regular evening back. Removing a move with the bin does the same: the regular evening is back.

To add an extra evening outside the rhythm, use **Add Proposed Date** and **Add Date**, as for any one-off session.

## "I can't make it"

Players say when they cannot come, without being asked. They can do it in two places:

- on their Hearth, in the **Next session** card: **I'm in** or **Can't make it**, under "Can you make it?";
- in their Settings, under **Upcoming Sessions**: press **Can't make it** on the evening.

Nothing is emailed. No answer means they are coming.

You see it in three places: beside the evening in **Upcoming Sessions** ("Wenna can't make it"), as a tally such as **3/4 available**, and on the dashboard's Next Session card. Use it to decide whether to **Move** or **Cancel evening**.

Absences only apply to regular evenings. For a proposed date, players answer yes or no (**Yes** and **No** in their Settings under **Session Availability**), and you see the dots beside each name.

### Answering from the email

A proposal email carries two answers: **I'm in** and **Can't make it**. A player can press one without signing in. It opens a small page asking them to confirm, and the answer is recorded only after they do. This is so a mail scanner opening the link cannot answer for them. Calendar apps that show Accept, Maybe and Decline on the invitation work too. Accept and Decline are recorded as the answer. **Maybe** records nothing.

## The calendar feed

At the bottom of the Scheduling tab, **Calendar Subscription** gives you a link that your calendar app can follow, so the schedule stays up to date by itself:

- Your regular evenings arrive as **one repeating event**.
- Confirmed dates arrive as normal events.
- Dates you have only suggested arrive as tentative events that leave the evening free.
- A moved or cancelled evening is taken out of the repeating event, so it changes in everyone's calendar.

Use **Copy** to copy the link, **Subscribe in Calendar App** to open it in your calendar, or **Download .ics** for a one-off snapshot that will not update. **Regenerate URL** makes a new link; anyone subscribed to the old one stops getting updates, so only use it if the link got to someone it should not have.

The feed is not offered on a young player's account.
