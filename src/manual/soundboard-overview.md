The Soundboard (`/soundboard`) holds the ambient tracks, music, and one-shot effects you cue up during a session.

## Sounds

- **Add Sound** uploads or links a track and files it under a category; free plans have a sound quota, shown on the button itself.
- Sounds live on **pages** — separate boards you can switch between (e.g. one page per dungeon, one for the tavern). Create additional pages from the page tabs at the top.
- Search and filter by category to find a sound fast mid-session.
- Drag cards to reorder them within a page; reordering is disabled while a filter is active so you don't lose track of position.

The **category** you give a sound is not just a label — it decides how the sound behaves in the mix. Music and Ambient sounds sit under everything else, while an Effects sound briefly dips them so a thunderclap or a door slam lands clearly instead of fighting the bed underneath it.

## The mixer

Open the floating widget and expand **Mixer** for a master fader plus one per group: Music, Ambience and Effects. Pulling Ambience down quiets every ambient layer at once without touching your music, which is much faster than riding each sound's own slider when the table gets loud.

## Trim: fixing sounds that are too loud or too quiet

Sounds from different sources arrive at wildly different levels — a field recording and a produced music track are rarely close. **Trim** on a sound card is the one-time correction for that: set it once and the app remembers, so you are not rebalancing the same sound every session.

Trim is deliberately separate from the volume slider. Volume is what you ride during play; trim is the correction underneath it. A sound you have adjusted shows a small gold badge so you can see at a glance which ones you have levelled.

## Effects

The wand icon on a sound applies a filter — through a door, through a wall, distant, underwater, cave or sewer — for when the party hears something from somewhere else. Effects fade in and out rather than snapping.

## Playlists

Switch the **Sounds / Playlists** toggle to build playlists from your library. There are two kinds, and the type is fixed when you create it:

- **Music** playlists play one track at a time and advance automatically, crossfading between tracks rather than cutting. Shuffle and repeat are available.
- **Ambient** playlists play every track at once as layers, each looping — a scene rather than a sequence.

## Spotify

If Spotify is connected on your account, use the **Connect Spotify** button to stream directly from your own account alongside your uploaded sounds, instead of only self-hosted files.

## Playing through other speakers

The **Cast** button sends a music playlist to a Chromecast or Google speaker, so the table hears it from the room's speakers instead of your laptop. Ambient scenes are layered locally and cannot be cast.

Music playlists also appear in your phone or car's own media controls (lock screen, CarPlay, Android Auto, Bluetooth buttons), showing the track name, artist and artwork, with play/pause and skip.

## The floating widget

The soundboard toggle in the top bar opens a small floating player so you can control playback while working in any other part of the app, without navigating back to this page. Drag it anywhere on screen. **Stop All** silences everything at once.
