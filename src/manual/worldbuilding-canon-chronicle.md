Two parts of the **Canon** page look back: the **Chronicle** (what happened) and **Backstories** (each character's past).

## The Chronicle

The **Chronicle** lists your sessions, with the session note you wrote for each. A note linked to a session is that session's chronicle entry. It also appears in your Notes as the same note, so there is only ever one copy.

Switch the order with **Newest first** or **From the start**. Entries load ten at a time; press **Show 10 more** for the rest.

Each entry shows:

- the session number and title, and the day you played it;
- the date **in the world**, if you set one on the note and use a calendar;
- who the note is shared with, or **Not shared yet**;
- the note's title (a link that opens it), the first picture in it and a short excerpt;
- **What the party learned**: the people, places, handouts, creatures and quests the party picked up that evening. This is read-only here. You sort it from the Sessions page.

A session with no note yet offers **Write the chronicle** to open the note editor and **Draft with the Chronicler** to have the Chronicler draft it from the session. A note that is not linked to a session never shows in the Chronicle.

### The almanac

Beside the Chronicle is **The almanac**: one timeline in the world's own calendar. It puts lore with a world year, force clock ticks with a world date, and every session (by its note's world date) in world order. Sessions without a world date come last. Lore and ticks with no date are not in it.

### What the table added

When the AI table plays a scene, it can put facts on record. They collect under **What the table added**, grouped by session and opening on **New**. For each fact you can:

- **Keep** it, so it stands as canon;
- **Edit** it, which files a correction;
- **Strike** it, so it never happened: players and the AI table stop reading it, though the line stays on screen with **Undo**;
- use the menu's **Make it a truth** to turn the fact into a truth in your canon.

A fact is reviewed once it is kept, struck or corrected. The filter reads **New**, **Kept**, **Struck** or **All**.

### Sorting old notes

If your campaign had lore notes and session notes from before the Canon, the Notes page shows a banner ("n notes to sort, from before lore and sessions moved to Canon") with a **Sort them** button. Send each note to a lore entry, a session, a character's backstory, your own note on a character, or keep it as a note. Nothing moves by itself.

## Backstories

A character's backstory is told in passages, and two people write it: the character's player and you. Open **Backstories** and choose a character to see it.

### Before play

The first part is **Before play**: the character's own Backstory text. It stays with the character, so it travels with them between campaigns and into the Hall of Heroes. Both you and the player can edit it, and it saves itself.

### Passages

Everything after that is the campaign's. Under **Told in passages**:

- The player adds their own passages with **Add to my story** in their journal, and can tick **Share with the party**. A passage says who wrote it: "Written by Lotte · before play", "... after session 12", or "Your passage" to its own writer.
- You can edit every passage, the player's included, and add your own with **Add a passage of yours**. Your passages start **Not yet told**.
- **Tell <character>** reveals a passage to that character, and **Tell the party** reveals it to everyone. **Take back** hides it again.
- **Comes out at** lets you place a passage at a place, a person or a beat, to be told when the party gets there.

A player can only add, change and delete passages they wrote in their own character's backstory. They cannot place a passage or share it beyond their own party.

A player's **Share with the party** only reaches other characters once you have shared the whole backstory with them, using the entry's **Share** control. The list on the Backstories page shows "n not yet told" for each character.

### The people from this past

**The people from this past** holds the backstory's connections: the mentor, the rival, the family. **New NPC from this backstory** creates a hidden NPC, asks how they relate to the character (**Relationship**), then **Create NPC** opens them so you can fill them in. The NPC and the connection stay hidden from players until you share them.

For how a connection reads ("about", "known to"), see [Canon](#canon).

## Tips

- Ask each player to write a few lines before the first session, then tell the interesting ones one at a time.
- Use **Draft with the Chronicler** right after the session, while the evening is fresh.
