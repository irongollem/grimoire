The **World web** (**Web** in the sidebar, `/web`) draws the ties between everything in your campaign: people, factions, places, gods, items and quests. It replaced the Relationship Web, which knew only the ties between people.

## Links and records

Two kinds of tie sit behind every line and every list on this screen.

- A **link** is a typed tie and nothing more: *worships*, *controls*, *rival of*. A link can join any kind of thing to any other that the catalogue allows.
- A **record** is a tie that carries facts of its own: a faction membership with its role, where an NPC lives, a PC's patron deity, what someone carries, where an item is sold, a quest's giver. Records stay where they are on their own sheets; the web reads them.

Kin (parents, spouses, siblings) are drawn as lines too, because they join one person to one person and so never make a hub.

## The web at rest

At rest the web draws links and kin as lines. Records are not lines, because a guild with twenty-three members would be a star that pushes everyone else to the rim. They group instead.

**Shapes** say what a node is: a circle for a person (a gold ring and their face for a party member), a shield for a faction, a pin-square for a place, a ringed disc for a deity, a diamond for an item, a seal for a quest. NPC circles are coloured by their attitude to the party.

A faction, place, deity, item or quest is a node only when it has a link. People are always nodes, so you can find someone who has no ties yet.

**Line colour** says what a tie is about: bond, enmity, faith, place, holding or a faction's stance, with kin in its own colour. The key sits at the foot of the graph.

**Faction badges**: up to three small faction emblems on a person (and a "+N" for more), faded for memberships that are no longer active. Point at one to see the faction's name and the person's role; click it to open the faction. On a touch screen the first tap opens the badge and a second follows it.

## The filter bar

| Control | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| **Find…** | Narrows the graph to nodes whose name matches. |
| Kind chips | People, Party, Factions, Places, Deities, Items, Quests, each with how many are on the web. Click one to hide or show that kind. |
| **Group by** | Gather a faction's members, a place's residents or a deity's faithful inside a hull, and pull them together. See below. |
| **Any tie** | Show only one colour of line. |
| **Focus on…** | Find anything in the world, including a place or item with no links, and focus on it. |
| **Clear** | Appears once any filter is on. Filters last for the session, so opening a sheet from the graph and coming back keeps your view. |

### Group by

Pick **Faction**, **Place** or **Deity**, then which one. The members are drawn inside a soft boundary and gently pulled toward each other, and the group's own node sits on the boundary so its links still leave from it. A faction gathers its members, a place gathers the NPCs who live there and the PCs who are there, and a deity gathers everyone who worships or serves them, plus any PC whose patron they are. One group at a time keeps the picture readable.

## Focus

Click a node and choose **Focus**, or use **Focus on…**. The web is replaced by that node laid out in rings: the node in the middle, everything one step away around it with the reason written under each ("worships", "patron deity", "opposes"), and with **2 steps** a faded second ring of what those are tied to. Records are drawn here as dashed spokes, so a deity's page shows everyone who worships her, whether that is a link or a PC's patron deity. Focus on a guild and its members appear, because now they are the subject.

Tap any node to move the focus there. The trail above the picture lists where you have been; tap a name in it to walk back. **Whole web** returns to the graph.

The panel answers in words: under each heading, such as "Worships The Drowned Lady", are the things that do.

A sheet's **In the web** link opens the web already focused on that sheet.

## Tying things together

Choose **Connect…** on a node, then tap another node, or find anyone with the search in the panel. Pick what they are to each other from the list: the common types first as chips, the rest under **More…**, searchable. Choosing a record type, such as a membership or a home, writes to the record that already holds that kind of tie, so there is never a second way to say the same thing.

To change a tie, click its line (or its pencil in the panel). You can pick a different type, change the line players are told, or remove the tie. Quest ties are changed on the quest.

## On a phone

A phone opens on a focus, never on two hundred dots: one ring, then the same ties as a list you can tap. Every row is a 44 pixel target. **Whole web** opens the pan-and-zoom graph when you want it.

## Moving around the graph

- **Drag a node** to place it; it stays where you drop it.
- **Scroll** to pan. **Ctrl/Cmd/Alt+scroll** or a pinch zooms. Middle-drag or hold **Space** and drag to pan with the mouse.
- Zoom keys work while the pointer is over the graph.

## What your players see

Players have their own **World web** (`/play/web`). It opens on their own character and shows only what that character knows: a link appears when you have revealed it to them and they can see both ends of it, and they read the line you wrote for players, never your note. A record follows the rule its sheet already has, so a membership shows when the faction is known and a home when the place is. They cannot edit anything, and nothing says how many ties are hidden.
